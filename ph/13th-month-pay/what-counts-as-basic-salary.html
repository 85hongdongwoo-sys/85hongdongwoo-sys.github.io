What counts as basic salary for 13th month pay?

Overtime, night diff, COLA, allowances, commissions — what goes in and what stays out.

Hong Life › Philippines › 13th month pay › What counts as basic salary

Only basic salary counts — the pay for the work you were hired to do in your regular hours, including days on paid leave. DOLE's advisories exclude overtime, premium and holiday pay, night shift differential, COLA and the cash value of unused leave, unless your contract, CBA or company practice treats them as part of basic pay.

The “unless” clause that matters

The exclusions are a floor, not a ceiling. If your contract says the 13th month will be computed on “gross compensation”, or your employer has for years included COLA or a fixed allowance, that becomes an enforceable benefit. The Labor Code's non-diminution rule (Article 100) stops employers from unilaterally withdrawing benefits that have ripened into company practice — generally ones given deliberately and consistently over a long period, not by a one-off payroll error.

Commissions: read how you're paid

Commissions are the grey zone. In Philippine Duplicators v. NLRC (1993, affirmed on reconsideration in 1995), salesmen's commissions — a percentage of each sale, paid as part of their regular pay — were counted in basic salary. In Boie-Takeda v. De la Serna (1993), productivity bonuses for medical reps were not. Employees paid purely on commission are listed in the 1987 guidelines as outside PD 851. If you have a fixed basic plus commissions, ask HR how payroll treats them, in writing.

COLA and wage orders

Some regional wage orders grant increases as COLA, and some later integrate COLA into the basic wage. Once it's integrated, it's basic salary and counts. If your payslip still shows a separate COLA line, it's excluded.

FAQ

Is overtime included in 13th month pay?

No. Overtime pay is excluded from basic salary under DOLE guidelines, unless a contract, CBA or company practice includes it.

Is COLA included in 13th month pay?

No. Cost of living allowance is listed among the exclusions, unless it has been integrated into basic salary by agreement, practice or a wage order.

Are allowances included in 13th month pay?

Generally no. Rice, transport, meal and clothing allowances are not basic salary. Check your contract if a package is described as “inclusive of allowances”.

Are paid leaves included?

Yes. Salary paid for days on vacation, sick or service incentive leave is basic salary for those days. Only the cash conversion of unused leave is excluded.

Are commissions part of basic salary?

It depends on their nature. The Supreme Court counted sales commissions that were part of the wage structure (Philippine Duplicators, 1993/1995) but excluded productivity-type incentives (Boie-Takeda, 1993).
