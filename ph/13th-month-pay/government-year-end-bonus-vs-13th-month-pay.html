Government year-end bonus vs 13th month pay

Same idea, different law: how government workers’ bonuses compare with the private-sector 13th month.

Hong Life › Philippines › 13th month pay › Government year-end bonus

PD 851 doesn't apply to government. Instead, national government employees receive a year-end bonus equal to one month's basic pay (as of 31 October) plus a ₱5,000 cash gift, released with the first payroll in November, and a separate mid-year bonus in May. Together they work out to more than a private-sector 13th month.

Example: a teacher with ₱35,000 basic pay

Ma'am Joanna teaches in a public high school in Tarlac. Say her basic pay is ₱35,000 a month. In May she receives a mid-year bonus of ₱35,000; in early November, a year-end bonus of ₱35,000 and the ₱5,000 cash gift. That's ₱75,000, all within the ₱90,000 tax-free ceiling.

Her sister works for a private BPO on the same ₱35,000 basic. Her 13th month is ₱35,000, plus whatever Christmas bonus the company decides to give.

Government personnel may also receive a Productivity Enhancement Incentive and a Performance-Based Bonus. Those go into the same ₱90,000 bucket, so higher-paid officials often end up with a taxable portion.

Eligibility details

  • Year-end bonus: at least four months of service from 1 January to 31 October, and still in service on 31 October. Personnel who left before 31 October may get a pro-rated amount under the conditions in the DBM circular.
  • Cash gift: ₱5,000, with the same eligibility; pro-rated for part-time and some contractual arrangements as specified by DBM.
  • Mid-year bonus: at least four months of service from 1 July of the previous year to 15 May, still in service on 15 May, and at least a “satisfactory” performance rating.

Job order and contract-of-service workers

JO and COS workers in government aren't employees and aren't covered by the year-end bonus rules or PD 851. Some agencies give them a gratuity under a separate DBM issuance when funds allow; it isn't guaranteed. Agency-supplied workers (security guards, janitors) are employees of their private contractor, which owes them a regular PD 851 13th month.

FAQ

Do government employees get 13th month pay?

Not under PD 851. They receive a year-end bonus of one month’s basic pay plus a ₱5,000 cash gift in November, and a mid-year bonus of one month’s basic pay in May.

When is the government year-end bonus released in 2026?

Under DBM Budget Circular No. 2024-3, the year-end bonus and cash gift are released with the first agency payroll in November, instead of the older “not earlier than 15 November” rule.

Who is eligible for the year-end bonus?

Personnel who have rendered at least four months of service from 1 January to 31 October and are still in government service on 31 October. Those with less service get a pro-rated amount under DBM rules.

Is the government year-end bonus taxable?

Only if the year-end bonus, mid-year bonus, cash gift and other benefits together exceed ₱90,000 for the year. The excess is taxed at the employee’s regular rate.

Is the mid-year bonus the same as a 14th month pay?

It is often called that informally. It is one month’s basic pay as of 15 May, paid to eligible government personnel with at least four months of service and a satisfactory performance rating.
