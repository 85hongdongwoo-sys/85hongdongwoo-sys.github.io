At ₱90,000 a month, a full-year 13th month pay is ₱90,000 — exactly the tax-free ceiling. It's fully exempt on its own, but any Christmas bonus or other benefit on top is taxed from the first peso.

A real-world example

Bea, an operations lead at a shared-services centre in Alabang on ₱90,000, gets her ₱90,000 13th month plus a ₱10,000 Christmas bonus and a ₱5,000 rice-and-grocery gift card.

Her 13th month uses up the whole ₱90,000 ceiling. The ₱10,000 bonus is fully taxable: at her 25% bracket, about ₱2,500. The gift card is a de minimis benefit if it stays within its own limit (the Christmas-gift item is ₱5,000 a year); anything beyond that limit would also join the taxable pile.

Right at the ceiling: what to watch

When your 13th month equals or exceeds ₱90,000, other bonuses lose their tax-free status entirely. A ₱20,000 performance bonus is worth about ₱15,000 in your pocket instead of ₱20,000.

If you started mid-year, though, your pro-rated 13th month is lower and there's room again: hired on 1 April, your 13th month is ₱67,500, leaving ₱22,500 of tax-free room for bonuses.

Is it taxed?

The 13th month alone fills the ₱90,000 ceiling exactly. Every peso of other bonuses is taxable — a ₱10,000 bonus costs about ₱2,500 at 25%.

The 13th month is never subject to SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG contributions. Tax estimates assume a full year at ₱90,000 with employee contributions of about ₱4,200 a month deducted.

What unpaid absences do to it

Quick tips

Bonuses negotiated as “net of tax” are worth more at this level: ask whether a sign-on or retention bonus is gross or net.
Payday falls on a weekend or holiday? 24 December 2026 is a Thursday, so there's no excuse for “we'll pay after Christmas”. Employers may — and many do — pay earlier.
Money claims, including unpaid 13th month pay, prescribe after three years. If an old employer still owes you for 2024, act before the end of 2027.

FAQ

Is ₱90,000 13th month pay taxable?

No — it is exactly the tax-free ceiling. But any other bonus received in the same year (other than de minimis within its limits) is then fully taxable.

Are de minimis benefits counted in the ₱90,000?

Only the part above their own limits. De minimis items within their ceilings (for example Christmas gifts up to ₱5,000 a year) are separately exempt; the excess joins the ₱90,000 pool.

Is 13th month pay subject to SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG?

No. Contributions are based on regular monthly compensation; the 13th month is not part of it.

When is the 13th month pay deadline in 2026?

On or before 24 December 2026 under PD 851. Employers may pay earlier, and may pay half before the school year opens and the rest by 24 December.
