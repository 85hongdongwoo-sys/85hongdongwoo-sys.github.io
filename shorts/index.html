How to turn a long video into Shorts

  1. Drop your video. It opens instantly — nothing is uploaded.
  2. Pick the moments. Mark a start and end, auto-split into 59-second parts, or press Suggest best moments to find the liveliest speaking sections.
  3. Reframe. Choose blurred-background fit, full crop, or a top-video layout, then adjust zoom and position.
  4. Tighten it. Turn on Remove silences for jump cuts and use 1.1× speed to keep viewers watching.
  5. Add text. Write a hook title, generate AI captions and fix any words in the list.
  6. Change the sound. Record a new voice-over while the clip plays, drop in a narration file, or add background music.
  7. Export. Download MP4 (or WebM on browsers without MP4 recording) and the .srt caption file.

FAQ

Is it really free with no watermark?

Yes. No account, no credits, no watermark. Ads on this page keep it free.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Cutting, captions and export all run in your browser. Only the caption AI model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

No — downloading other people's YouTube videos breaks YouTube's terms and usually copyright. Use your own recordings, or download your own channel's videos from YouTube Studio (Content → ⋮ → Download).

Why does export take as long as the clip?

The browser records the finished frame in real time, the same way a screen recorder does. A 59-second Short takes about a minute. Keep the tab in front while it runs.

MP4 or WebM?

Recent Chrome and Edge export MP4 directly. Other browsers export WebM, which YouTube, TikTok and Instagram also accept.

How accurate are the captions?

The “Fast” model is fine for clear English speech; choose “Accurate” for accents, music in the background or other languages. You can edit every line before exporting.
