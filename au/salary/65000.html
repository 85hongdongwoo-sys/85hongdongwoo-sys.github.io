How a $65,000 salary is taxed in 2026-27

Income tax. The first $18,200 is tax-free, then 15% applies to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above. On $65,000 that adds up to $10,020. The low income tax offset cuts this by $25. Your top dollars fall in the 30% bracket, but your average income tax rate is only 15.4%.

Medicare levy. At this income the full 2% applies: $1,300. Without an appropriate level of private hospital cover, singles above the surcharge threshold ($101,000 in 2025-26, indexed each year) may also pay the Medicare levy surcharge of 1% to 1.5%, which is not included here.

HELP / HECS. Your income is below the 2026-27 HELP repayment threshold of $69,528, so a HELP or HECS debt costs nothing in compulsory repayments this year.

The 2026-27 tax cut. The 16% rate dropped to 15% on 1 July 2026, worth $268 a year ($10 a fortnight) at this salary. A further cut to 14% is legislated from 1 July 2027. A new $1,000 instant deduction for work-related expenses is planned from the 2026-27 income year; it is claimed at tax time and is not included in these figures.

A raise from here. Going from $65,000 to $70,000 adds $3,375 to take-home — about 68 cents of each extra dollar — plus $600 more super.

FAQ

How much is $65,000 after tax in Australia?

In 2026-27 a $65,000 salary leaves $53,705 a year after $9,995 income tax and a $1,300 Medicare levy, for a resident claiming the tax-free threshold. Your employer also pays $7,800 into super on top.

How much is $65,000 a year per fortnight after tax?

About $2,066 a fortnight, $4,475 a month or $1,032.79 a week. With a HELP debt the fortnightly pay is $2,066.

What is $65,000 a year per hour?

$65,000 is $32.89 an hour before tax for a 38-hour week (1,976 hours a year), and about $27.18 an hour after tax and Medicare.

How much more do I take home in 2026-27 than in 2025-26?

About $268 more a year, because the 16% rate on income between $18,201 and $45,000 fell to 15% from 1 July 2026. The other rates and thresholds did not change.

What tax bracket is $65,000 in?

The top dollars of $65,000 fall in the 30% bracket. The marginal rate including the 2% Medicare levy is 34%.
