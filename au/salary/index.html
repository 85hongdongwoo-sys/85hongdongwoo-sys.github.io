FAQ

How is take-home pay calculated in Australia?

Take-home pay is salary minus income tax (after the low income tax offset), the 2% Medicare levy and, if you have one, compulsory HELP/HECS repayments. Employer super of 12% is paid on top of your salary and is not deducted from it.

How much is $80,000 after tax in 2026-27?

About $63,880 a year, $2,457 a fortnight or $5,323 a month for a resident with no HELP debt, plus $9,600 super.

How much is $100,000 after tax in Australia?

About $77,480 a year ($6,457 a month) in 2026-27, or $72,909 with a HELP debt.

What are the tax rates for 2026-27?

For residents: nil up to $18,200, 15% from $18,201 to $45,000 (cut from 16%), 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above, plus the 2% Medicare levy. The 15% rate falls to 14% from 1 July 2027.
