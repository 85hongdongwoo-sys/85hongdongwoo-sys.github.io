Hourly to annual salary in Australia: $27 an hour by pay period

To convert an hourly rate into an annual salary in Australia, multiply it by your weekly hours and by 52. The National Employment Standards set a maximum of 38 ordinary hours a week for full-time staff, so $27 × 38 × 52 = $53,352. A day is 7.6 hours; paid annual leave and public holidays are already inside the 52 weeks for permanent employees. Most Australians are paid fortnightly, so that column is the one to check against your payslip.

How $27 an hour is taxed in 2026-27

From 1 July 2026 the first resident tax rate fell from 16% to 15% (rates: nil to $18,200; 15% to $45,000; 30% to $135,000; 37% to $190,000; 45% above). On $53,352 that means $26,800 at 15%, $8,352 at 30% — $6,526 before offsets. The low income tax offset then removes $200, leaving $6,326. The 2% Medicare levy adds $1,067. Compared with 2025-26 rates, the 15% cut puts $268 a year ($10 a fortnight) back in your pocket. Your marginal rate on the next dollar is 34% including Medicare.

You pay no income tax until yearly income passes $18,200 — at $27 that is 13.0 hours a week. HELP repayments start at 49.5 hours a week ($69,528), so a full-time week on this rate never triggers them without overtime. The 37% bracket ($135,000+) is out of reach at any realistic number of hours.

Just above the minimum. $27 is $0.56 an hour (2%) above the $26.44 national minimum wage — worth $1,107 a year before tax on 38-hour weeks — but $15.90 below the ABS median hourly pay of $42.90. Many retail, hospitality and care awards sit in this range for permanent staff. At $53,352 a year your top dollars are taxed at 30% plus the 2% Medicare levy, and you still receive $200 of the low income tax offset.

Casual loading: is $33.75 really more?

A casual employee gets a 25% loading instead of paid annual leave (4 weeks), personal/carer’s leave (10 days) and notice. At $27 base that means $33.75 an hour. Work 38 hours for a full 52 weeks and the casual earns $66,690 — $13,338 more than a permanent — but take the same 6 weeks off unpaid and the casual year is $58,995, still $5,643 ahead of the permanent’s $53,352. Super is 12% of ordinary pay for both.

HELP / HECS repayments on $27 an hour

From the 2026-27 income year, compulsory HELP repayments use a marginal system: nothing up to $69,528 of repayment income, then 15c for each dollar above it, $9,028 plus 17c per dollar above $129,717, and 10% of total income above $186,050 (ATO). At 38 hours your $53,352 is below the threshold, so a HELP debt is not repaid through payroll — but indexation still applies each 1 June. Repayment income also adds reportable fringe benefits, reportable super contributions and net investment losses, so salary sacrifice does not dodge HELP.

Tips to keep more of $27 an hour

Overtime and penalty rates. Each extra ordinary hour at $27 adds about $17.96 after tax and Medicare. Award overtime is often paid at 150% or 200%, so check your payslip shows the right multiplier.
Keep receipts for work deductions. Work-related expenses such as uniforms, tools, union fees and some car or phone costs can reduce taxable income. Claims need records and must be directly related to earning your income.
Run your exact payslip. Different hours each fortnight, salary sacrifice, HELP or a package that includes super are all handled by the Australian pay calculator.
Claim the tax-free threshold once. Tick “yes” to the $18,200 tax-free threshold on your TFN declaration for your main job only. With two jobs, claiming it on both usually leaves a tax bill at the end of the year.
Watch your super land — Payday Super. From 1 July 2026 employers must pay the 12% super guarantee at the same time as wages and it must reach your fund within 7 business days. At $27 on 38 hours that is about $246 a fortnight — check your fund app matches your payslip.

FAQ

$27 an hour is how much a year?

At 38 hours a week for 52 weeks (1,976 hours), $27 an hour is $53,352 a year before tax. At 30 hours it is $42,120 and at 20 hours $28,080.

How much is $27 an hour after tax in Australia?

In 2026-27 a full-time $27 an hour job leaves $45,959 a year after $6,326 income tax and a $1,067 Medicare levy — $1,768 a fortnight, $884 a week or about $23.26 an hour. Your employer also pays $6,402 super.

How much is $27 an hour per fortnight after tax?

About $1,768 a fortnight on 38 hours a week. Gross fortnightly pay is $2,052.

Is $27 an hour a good wage in Australia?

$27 is 2% above the $26.44 national minimum wage and 37% below the $42.90 median hourly earnings reported by the ABS for August 2025.

What is $27 an hour as a casual?

With the 25% casual loading, a $27 base rate becomes $33.75 an hour. Full time that is $66,690 a year and $54,829 after tax, but casuals get no paid annual or personal leave.

How much HELP do I repay on $27 an hour?

Nothing on 38 hours a week, because $53,352 is below the 2026-27 HELP repayment threshold of $69,528.
