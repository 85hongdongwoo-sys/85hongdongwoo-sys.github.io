The 28/36 rule says housing (PITI) should stay under 28% of gross monthly income and all debt payments under 36%. If you already pay $500 a month on other debts, the 36% test needs $142,680 of income for the 30-year loan. Lenders may approve higher ratios (often up to 43%–50% debt-to-income), but the 28/36 guideline is a comfortable target. See what that income means after tax on the salary after-tax page.

FAQ

What is the monthly payment on a $450,000 mortgage?

At the Freddie Mac average 30-year fixed rate of 7.24% (week of October 29, 2026), principal and interest on $450,000 is $3,067 a month. A 15-year loan at 6.60% is $3,945 a month. Adding estimated property tax ($422) and homeowners insurance ($292) brings the 30-year payment to about $3,780.

How much income do I need for a $450,000 mortgage?

Using the 28% housing guideline, a $3,780 monthly housing payment (30-year, with tax and insurance) needs about $162,018 of gross annual income ($13,501 a month). Under the 36% total-debt guideline, that income leaves room for about $1,080 a month of car, student-loan and card payments.

How much interest will I pay on a $450,000 mortgage?

Over 30 years at 7.24% you would pay about $654,027 in interest, 145% of the amount borrowed. A 15-year loan at 6.60% costs about $260,057 in interest, saving $393,970.

How much does 1% on the rate change a $450,000 payment?

On a 30-year loan of $450,000, each additional percentage point adds roughly $311 a month near today's rates. For example, the payment is $2,698 at 6% and $2,994 at 7%.

What house price does a $450,000 mortgage buy?

With 20% down, a $450,000 loan buys a home of about $562,500, with a down payment of $112,500. With less than 20% down, lenders usually add private mortgage insurance (PMI), which raises the monthly cost.

  • Rates: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, week of October 29, 2026 — 30-year fixed 7.24% (prior week 7.32%, a year earlier 6.17%), 15-year fixed 6.60%. PMMS rates are national averages for borrowers with strong credit and 20% down; quotes vary.
