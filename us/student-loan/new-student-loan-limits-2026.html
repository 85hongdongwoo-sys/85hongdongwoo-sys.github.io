New Student Loan Limits From July 2026

The 2025 law ended open-ended graduate and parent borrowing. Here are the new annual and lifetime caps, who is grandfathered, and what a maxed-out balance costs per month.

Short answer

For loans made on or after July 1, 2026, Grad PLUS is closed to new borrowers. Graduate students can borrow up to $20,500 a year ($100,000 total); professional students (medicine, law, etc.) up to $50,000 a year ($200,000 total). Parent PLUS is capped at $20,000 a year per student and $65,000 total, and there is a $257,500 lifetime limit on federal student loans. Undergraduate limits did not change.

Annual vs lifetime caps: worked examples

Both limits apply: you can’t exceed the annual cap in any year or the aggregate cap in total. Some common paths under the new rules:

A four-year medical program at the professional annual cap hits the $200,000 aggregate exactly, so any undergraduate debt or earlier graduate loans count against the separate $257,500 lifetime limit. Programs costing more than the caps will need savings, school aid or private loans to close the gap.

How students are filling the gap

  • Private student loans — credit-based rates, usually with a cosigner; no RAP, PSLF or federal forgiveness.
  • Institutional aid and payment plans — some schools are expanding grants for graduate programs that cost more than the cap.
  • Cheaper programs — the cap makes total cost of attendance a bigger factor in choosing a school.
  • Employer tuition assistance — check your benefits before you borrow.
Mind the order: borrowing federal loans first keeps access to RAP and PSLF; private loans fill only what remains.

Frequently asked questions

How much can graduate students borrow in 2026?

From July 1, 2026, new graduate borrowers can take up to $20,500 a year and $100,000 in total in federal Direct Unsubsidized Loans.

Is Grad PLUS eliminated?

Grad PLUS is closed to new borrowers for loans made on or after July 1, 2026, with a limited exception for students already enrolled in a program.

What is the new Parent PLUS limit?

$20,000 per year per dependent student and $65,000 in total per student.

Did undergraduate loan limits change?

No. Dependent undergraduates can still borrow up to $31,000 in total and independent undergraduates $57,500, though limits are now prorated for less-than-full-time enrollment.

What is the lifetime federal student loan limit?

$257,500 across all federal student loans.
