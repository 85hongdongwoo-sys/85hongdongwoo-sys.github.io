Frequently asked questions

What is the average car loan interest rate in 2026?

Experian's Q2 2026 data puts the average APR at 6.35% for new-vehicle loans and 11.19% for used-vehicle loans. Superprime borrowers averaged 4.41% (new), deep subprime 16.11%.

What is the average car payment?

About $765 a month for new vehicles and $542 for used vehicles in Q2 2026 (Experian), on average loans of $43,610 and $27,852.

How much car can I afford?

A common guideline is 20/4/10: 20% down, a loan of no more than four years, and payment plus insurance under 10% of gross income. On $75,000 a year that is roughly a $19,000 loan at 6.35% APR over 48 months.

How much does it cost to own a car per month?

AAA's 2026 Your Driving Costs study puts the average at $12,863 a year (about $1,072 a month) for a new vehicle driven 15,000 miles a year, including depreciation, insurance, fuel, maintenance, finance charges and fees.
