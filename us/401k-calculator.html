Never leave the match on the table

A typical match — 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary — is an instant 50% return. Contribute at least enough to get the full match before investing anywhere else.

Roth or Traditional?

Traditional contributions save tax now and are taxed when withdrawn; Roth contributions are taxed now and grow tax-free. With the same budget, the two give the same result when your tax rate is unchanged. If you expect a lower rate in retirement, Traditional wins; if higher, Roth wins. Many people split contributions to diversify.

FAQ

What is the 401(k) contribution limit for 2026?

$24,500 for employees, plus an $8,000 catch-up at age 50 or older, or an $11,250 super catch-up at ages 60 to 63.

How much should I have in my 401(k) by age?

A common guideline is 1× your salary by 30, 3× by 40, 6× by 50 and 8× by 60, counting all retirement savings.

Is a Roth 401(k) better than Traditional?

Roth is better if your tax rate in retirement will be higher than today; Traditional is better if it will be lower. The calculator compares both using your rates.
