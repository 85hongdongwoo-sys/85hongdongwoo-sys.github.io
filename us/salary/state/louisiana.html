How Louisiana compares

At $50,000 Louisiana takes $1,114 in state income tax, at $100,000 $2,614 and at $150,000 $4,114. Compared with a state with no income tax, that is $218 less take-home per month at $100,000. At $75,000 Louisiana ranks 13 of 51 (1 = keeps the most).

Federal taxes are the same in every state: the 2026 standard deduction is $16,100, brackets run from 10% to 37%, Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500 and Medicare 1.45% (plus 0.9% above $200,000).
