Ohio income tax rules used

No tax on the first $26,050 of Ohio taxable income, then $332 + 2.75% of the excess (flat rate from 2026); personal exemption $2,400 / $2,150 / $1,900 depending on income.

Local & payroll taxes: Most Ohio cities levy a municipal income tax (typically 1%–2.5%), and some school districts add more — not included.

Source: Ohio Rev. Code §5747.02(A)(3)(c) & §5747.025; Tax Foundation 2026.

How Ohio compares
