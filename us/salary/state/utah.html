How Utah compares

At $50,000 Utah takes $1,672 in state income tax, at $100,000 $4,450 and at $150,000 $6,675. Compared with a state with no income tax, that is $371 less take-home per month at $100,000. At $75,000 Utah ranks 36 of 51 (1 = keeps the most).

Federal taxes are the same in every state: the 2026 standard deduction is $16,100, brackets run from 10% to 37%, Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500 and Medicare 1.45% (plus 0.9% above $200,000).
