What changed for 2026 refunds

The 2026 tax year is the first full year in which payroll systems, W-2s and the W-4 all reflect the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Public Law 119-21, signed July 4, 2025). The standard deduction is $16,100 single, $32,200 married filing jointly and $24,150 head of household; the Child Tax Credit is $2,200 per child (up to $1,700 refundable); and four temporary deductions — tips, overtime, age 65+ and new-car loan interest — are claimed on Schedule 1-A.

Refunds in the 2026 filing season already rose: the IRS reported an average refund of $3,273 through May 1, 2026, up 11.1% from $2,947 a year earlier. Whether yours grows in 2027 depends on one thing: did your W-4 already account for these breaks? If not, the savings were left in your withholding and come back in the spring.

Tax refund estimator FAQ

How is my tax refund calculated?

Refund = federal income tax withheld from your paychecks − your total tax after non-refundable credits + refundable credits such as the Additional Child Tax Credit and the EITC. If the result is negative, you owe that amount by April 15, 2027.

Will refunds be bigger in 2027?

For many people, yes. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act added deductions for tips (up to $25,000), overtime premium (up to $12,500, or $25,000 joint), age 65+ ($6,000 per person) and new-car loan interest (up to $10,000), and raised the Child Tax Credit to $2,200. Unless you added these to your W-4, the tax savings come back as a refund.

How accurate is this estimator?

It models one or two W-2 incomes with the 2026 brackets, standard deduction, Child Tax Credit, EITC and the four new Schedule 1-A deductions. It ignores itemized deductions, investment income, education credits and state taxes. Treat it as an estimate, not tax advice.

What if I don't know how much was withheld?

Leave the withholding box blank and the estimator assumes a W-4 with only the filing-status box checked (IRS Publication 15-T 2026 standard tables). For a real number, add up the federal income tax line on your pay stubs or wait for Box 2 of your W-2.

When will I get my 2026 tax refund?

The IRS says most e-filed returns with direct deposit are refunded within 21 days. Returns claiming the EITC or Additional Child Tax Credit cannot be refunded before mid-February by law (the PATH Act hold). The 2027 filing-season start date had not been announced as of November 3, 2026.
