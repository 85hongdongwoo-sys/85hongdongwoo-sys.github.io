EITC 2026 amounts and income limits

The Earned Income Tax Credit is the largest refundable credit for working families — and the reason many refunds are held until mid-February.

Short answer

For tax year 2026 the maximum EITC is $664 with no qualifying children, $4,427 with one, $7,316 with two and $8,231 with three or more. It phases out completely at about $19,540 to $62,974 of income for single and head-of-household filers and $26,820 to $70,244 for married couples filing jointly. Investment income must be $12,200 or less.

Three examples

Head of household, $28,000, two kids. Phase-out begins at $23,890, so the credit is $7,316 − 21.06% × $4,110 ≈ $6,450. Add the refundable child credit ($3,400) and her estimated refund is about $10,235 — far more than the $385 withheld.

Married, $52,000, three kids. The joint phase-out starts at $31,160, so they keep about $3,842 of EITC. Refund with standard W-4s: about $10,442.

Single, $15,000, no kids, age 30. The small worker credit is about $347; with withholding of $0, the refund is roughly $347. Without children, you must be 25–64 and not someone else’s dependent.

Rules that trip people up

  • Investment income (interest, dividends, capital gains, rents) above $12,200 disqualifies you entirely.
  • Married filing separately can claim it only in limited separation situations.
  • The tips and overtime deductions don’t reduce AGI, so they won’t increase your EITC. Pre-tax 401(k) contributions don’t either, because the credit uses earned income and AGI, whichever gives the smaller credit.
  • Qualifying children must meet age, relationship and residency tests and have SSNs. A child under 19 (24 if a full-time student) or permanently disabled qualifies.
  • PATH Act timing: returns claiming the EITC can’t be refunded before mid-February. In 2026 most early filers were paid around March 2.

About one in five eligible workers never claims the EITC, according to the IRS — often because they earn too little to have to file. If you worked in 2026 and earned under about $63,000, it’s worth checking. Related: Child Tax Credit 2026, refund on $40,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum EITC for 2026?

$8,231 for three or more qualifying children, $7,316 for two, $4,427 for one and $664 with no children.

What are the EITC income limits for 2026?

For single or head of household filers the credit ends at about $19,540 (no children), $51,593 (one), $58,629 (two) and $62,974 (three+). For married filing jointly: $26,820, $58,863, $65,899 and about $70,244.

What is the EITC investment income limit for 2026?

$12,200. Above that, you can’t claim the credit.

When will I get my EITC refund in 2027?

Not before mid-February because of the PATH Act. In 2026, most e-filed EITC refunds with direct deposit arrived around March 2.

Does no tax on tips or overtime increase my EITC?

No. Those deductions reduce taxable income, not AGI, so the EITC is unchanged.
