The minimum down payment in Canada is 5% of the first $500,000 of the price and 10% of the portion between $500,000 and $1.5 million; homes of $1.5 million or more need 20%. A $750,000 home therefore needs $50,000, and anything under 20% down requires mortgage default insurance.

Minimum down payment rules (2026)

  • $500,000 or less: 5% of the price.
  • $500,000 to $1,499,999: 5% of the first $500,000 ($25,000) + 10% of the rest.
  • $1.5 million or more: 20% — insured mortgages are not available.

The upper limit was raised from $1 million to $1.5 million on 15 December 2024; before that, any home of $1 million or more needed 20% down. The formula itself did not change.

Where the down payment can come from

  • Savings and investments — lenders usually want to see 90 days of history.
  • First Home Savings Account (FHSA) — up to $8,000 a year and $40,000 lifetime, tax-deductible going in and tax-free coming out for a qualifying first home.
  • RRSP Home Buyers’ Plan — first-time buyers can withdraw up to $60,000 each (raised from $35,000 in April 2024) and repay it over 15 years.
  • Gifts from immediate family, with a signed gift letter stating it does not need to be repaid.
  • Borrowed funds — some insurers accept a “non-traditional” down payment, but the premium rises to 4.50%.

Minimum down vs 10% vs 20%

On a $700,000 home the minimum $45,000 leads to a $681,200 mortgage after the 4.00% premium — $3,939 a month at 4.94%. With 10% down ($70,000) the premium falls to 3.10% and the payment to $3,756; with 20% ($140,000) there is no premium and the payment is $3,238.

Don’t forget closing costs. Lenders expect roughly 1.5% of the price in addition to the down payment for land transfer tax, legal fees and adjustments.

FAQ

What is the minimum down payment in Canada?

5% of the purchase price up to $500,000, plus 10% of the portion from $500,000 to $1.5 million. Homes priced at $1.5 million or more require at least 20% down.

How much down payment do I need for a $750,000 house?

$50,000: $25,000 on the first $500,000 plus $25,000 (10%) on the remaining $250,000.

How much down payment for a $1 million house in Canada?

$75,000 since 15 December 2024 ($25,000 + 10% of $500,000). Before that date a $1 million home required 20%, or $200,000.

Can I use my RRSP for a down payment?

Yes. Through the Home Buyers’ Plan each first-time buyer can withdraw up to $60,000 from an RRSP and repay it over 15 years.

Do I need CMHC insurance with 20% down?

No. Mortgage default insurance is mandatory only when the down payment is less than 20%.
