Why Canadian mortgages are different

By law, fixed-rate mortgages in Canada quote interest compounded semi-annually, so the true monthly rate is slightly lower than the posted rate divided by 12.

CMHC insurance

With less than 20% down you must buy mortgage default insurance: 4.00% of the loan for 5–9.99% down, 3.10% for 10–14.99% and 2.80% for 15–19.99%. It is added to the mortgage. Since December 2024, first-time buyers and buyers of new builds can choose a 30-year amortization on insured mortgages.

FAQ

How do I calculate a Canadian mortgage payment?

Convert the posted rate to an effective annual rate with semi-annual compounding, then to a monthly rate, and use the standard annuity formula.

What is an accelerated bi-weekly payment?

Half your monthly payment every two weeks — 26 payments a year, equal to one extra monthly payment.

How much is CMHC insurance?

2.8% to 4.0% of the loan depending on your down payment.
