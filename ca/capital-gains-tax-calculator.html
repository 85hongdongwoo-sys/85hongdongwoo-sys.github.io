How capital gains are taxed in Canada

Only 50% of a capital gain is taxable. That half is added to your income and taxed at your marginal rate, so the effective rate is half your marginal rate — up to about 26.8% in Ontario.

The increase to a two-thirds inclusion rate announced in 2024 was cancelled in March 2025. Capital losses can offset gains and be carried back three years or forward indefinitely.

TFSA

Gains inside a TFSA are tax-free. The 2026 TFSA limit is $7,000.
