Our approach

IQ is a well-studied measure that predicts some outcomes on average, and it is easy to misuse. We therefore show ranges and uncertainty rather than single numbers, cite peer-reviewed sources and test manuals, skip occupations where reliable data are not available, and do not publish rankings of countries or ethnic groups. Nothing here is a diagnosis; for a valid score, see a qualified psychologist.
