How it works

The refund includes your contributions and your employer’s, plus interest. Contribution rates: 9% until 2025, 9.5% in 2026 and 10% in 2027 (split half and half).

Eligibility depends on your nationality and social security agreements. Apply at an NPS branch or at the airport before departure.

Frequently asked questions

Who can claim the refund?
Nationals of countries with refund agreements or reciprocity (e.g. USA, Canada, Australia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines), and E-8, E-9, H-2 visa holders.
How long does it take?
Usually about a month after applying.
Can I claim it while still in Korea?
No, only after leaving Korea or when your stay ends.
