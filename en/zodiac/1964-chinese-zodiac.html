When did the 1964 Year of the Dragon start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1964 that date was February 13, 1964.

In 1964 Ipchun (Feb 5) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 13), so babies born from Feb 5 to Feb 12 have a 甲辰 (Wood Dragon) year pillar in Saju but are still Rabbits in the popular zodiac.

Wood Dragon meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1964 is stem 甲 (Gap / Jiǎ, Yang Wood) on branch 辰 (Jin, the Dragon), number 41 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Wood Dragon” rather than just “Dragon”.

Reading the element. Wood is the element of spring and growth. Wood years are traditionally linked with generosity, cooperation, a strong sense of fairness and a drive to improve things step by step. The flip side is that Wood people can spread themselves thin, start more projects than they finish, or become stubborn when their ideals are questioned. For a Wood year the keywords are growth, kindness and idealism. The Dragon itself carries Earth as its fixed element, so this year blends wood energy with earth qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1964 and 1965 are Lamp Fire (覆燈火), pictured as a covered lamp burning at night: insight, guidance for others and a light that is best protected from the wind.

The same 甲辰 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gap-Jin day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1964 is called the year of the 푸른 용띠 (blue/green dragon). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1964 counts as tatsu (辰, Dragon) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1964 Wood Dragon personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: ambitious, charismatic, energetic, proud + growth, kindness and idealism

Tradition sees the Dragon as larger than life: idealistic, energetic and lucky, with a pride that pushes it to great things and occasionally into great arguments.

The only mythical creature in the cycle, the dragon was the symbol of emperors, rain and good fortune. Dragon years are still popular for births in Chinese communities, a well-documented "dragon baby" effect in places such as Taiwan, Hong Kong and Singapore.

Where the Wood element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Wood Dragon is cooperative, principled, keeps growing. Compared with the Water Dragon of 1952 and the Fire Dragon of 1976, the 1964 version is read as the most growth-minded and cooperative Dragon.

FAQ: 1964 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1964?

1964 is the Year of the Yang Wood Dragon (甲辰, Jiǎ Chén). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 13, 1964 and ended on Feb 1, 1965, so people born in that window are Dragons. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1964. Am I a Dragon?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 13, 1964. Births from January 1 to Feb 12, 1964 belong to the Yin Water Rabbit of 1963. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 5, 1964 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1964 Dragon?

Traditionally the Rooster is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Monkey and Rat (Three Harmony). The Dog is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Rabbit forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1964 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1964 turns 62 during 2026 and 63 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1964 Wood Dragon?

No special relationship with the Goat. Your birth-year element, Wood, generates Fire. Tradition reads this as an output year: ideas, creativity and giving to others flow easily, but energy can drain, so rest and boundaries matter. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1964 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1964 is 갑진-nyeon (갑진년, Gap-Jin), the year of the 용, so people born then are 푸른 용띠 — literally a blue/green dragon, from the Wood colour of the Wood stem.
