If you were born between Jan 22, 2004 and Feb 8, 2005, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Monkey — more precisely the Yang Wood Monkey (甲申). Births earlier in 2004, from January 1 to Jan 21, 2004, still belong to the previous sign, the Yin Water Goat (2003).

When did the 2004 Year of the Monkey start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 2004 that date was January 22, 2004.

Because Lunar New Year (Jan 22) came before Ipchun (Feb 4) in 2004, babies born from Jan 22 to Feb 3 are Monkeys by the popular zodiac but still have a 癸未 (Water Goat) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Wood Monkey meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 2004 is stem 甲 (Gap / Jiǎ, Yang Wood) on branch 申 (Sin, the Monkey), number 21 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Wood Monkey” rather than just “Monkey”.

Reading the element. Wood is the element of spring and growth. Wood years are traditionally linked with generosity, cooperation, a strong sense of fairness and a drive to improve things step by step. The flip side is that Wood people can spread themselves thin, start more projects than they finish, or become stubborn when their ideals are questioned. For a Wood year the keywords are growth, kindness and idealism. The Monkey itself carries Metal as its fixed element, so this year blends wood energy with metal qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 2004 and 2005 are Spring Water (泉中水), pictured as water rising from a spring: freshness, inner resources and ideas that keep flowing when others run dry.

The same 甲申 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gap-Sin day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 2004 is called the year of the 푸른 원숭이띠 (blue/green monkey). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 2004 counts as saru (申, Monkey) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

2004 Wood Monkey personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: clever, curious, playful, inventive + growth, kindness and idealism

Tradition sees the Monkey as the inventor of the zodiac: quick, playful and endlessly inventive, at its best when it has a new puzzle to crack.

The Monkey King, Sun Wukong of Journey to the West, gave the sign its reputation for brilliance, mischief and endless tricks. Japan’s three wise monkeys (see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil) are another monkey motif.

Where the Wood element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Wood Monkey is cooperative, principled, keeps growing. Compared with the Water Monkey of 1992 and the Fire Monkey of 2016, the 2004 version is read as the most growth-minded and cooperative Monkey.

FAQ: 2004 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 2004?

2004 is the Year of the Yang Wood Monkey (甲申, Jiǎ Shēn). The year began at Lunar New Year on Jan 22, 2004 and ended on Feb 8, 2005, so people born in that window are Monkeys. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 2004. Am I a Monkey?

Only if you were born on or after Jan 22, 2004. Births from January 1 to Jan 21, 2004 belong to the Yin Water Goat of 2003. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 2004 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 2004 Monkey?

Traditionally the Snake is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rat and Dragon (Three Harmony). The Tiger is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Pig forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 2004 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 2004 turns 22 during 2026 and 23 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 2004 Wood Monkey?

No special relationship with the Goat. Your birth-year element, Wood, generates Fire. Tradition reads this as an output year: ideas, creativity and giving to others flow easily, but energy can drain, so rest and boundaries matter. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 2004 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 2004 is 갑신-nyeon (갑신년, Gap-Sin), the year of the 원숭이, so people born then are 푸른 원숭이띠 — literally a blue/green monkey, from the Wood colour of the Wood stem.
