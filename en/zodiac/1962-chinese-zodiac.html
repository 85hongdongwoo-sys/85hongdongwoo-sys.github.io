1962 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Water Tiger

If you were born between Feb 5, 1962 and Jan 24, 1963, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Tiger — more precisely the Yang Water Tiger (壬寅). Births earlier in 1962, from January 1 to Feb 4, 1962, still belong to the previous sign, the Yin Metal Ox (1961).

When did the 1962 Year of the Tiger start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1962 that date was February 5, 1962.

In 1962 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 5), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 4 have a 壬寅 (Water Tiger) year pillar in Saju but are still Oxs in the popular zodiac.

Water Tiger meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1962 is stem 壬 (Im / Rén, Yang Water) on branch 寅 (In, the Tiger), number 39 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Water Tiger” rather than just “Tiger”.

Reading the element. Water is the element of winter, depth and flow. Water years are associated with intelligence, intuition, diplomacy and the ability to adapt to any container. Tradition warns of over-flexibility: Water can drift, keep options open too long or hide its real feelings. For a Water year the keywords are wisdom, adaptability and communication. The Tiger itself carries Wood as its fixed element, so this year blends water energy with wood qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1962 and 1963 are Gold Leaf Metal (金箔金), pictured as gold beaten into thin leaf: refinement, taste and value that shines when it is applied to something else.

The same 壬寅 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Im-In day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1962 is called the year of the 검은 호랑이띠 (black tiger). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1962 counts as tora (寅, Tiger) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1962 Water Tiger personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: brave, confident, competitive, generous + wisdom, adaptability and communication

In tradition the Tiger is bold and magnetic: it acts on conviction, protects its own fiercely and prefers freedom to rules it did not write.

The tiger was revered across Korea, China and Vietnam as the king of the mountains and a protector against evil, which is why tiger paintings were hung at the New Year. The Tiger month opens the solar year at Ipchun (Start of Spring).

Where the Water element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Water Tiger is perceptive, diplomatic, adaptable. Compared with the Metal Tiger of 1950 and the Wood Tiger of 1974, the 1962 version is read as the most adaptable and diplomatic Tiger.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1962

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a neutral year for the Water Tiger:

FAQ: 1962 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1962?

1962 is the Year of the Yang Water Tiger (壬寅, Rén Yín). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 5, 1962 and ended on Jan 24, 1963, so people born in that window are Tigers. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1962. Am I a Tiger?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 5, 1962. Births from January 1 to Feb 4, 1962 belong to the Yin Metal Ox of 1961. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1962 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1962 Tiger?

Traditionally the Pig is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Horse and Dog (Three Harmony). The Monkey is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Snake forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1962 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1962 turns 64 during 2026 and 65 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1962 Water Tiger?

No special relationship with the Goat. Water puts out Fire, so your element is the one in control. In traditional terms the year becomes a "wealth" year: opportunities you have to chase and manage, with gains that reflect the work invested. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1962 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1962 is 임인-nyeon (임인년, Im-In), the year of the 호랑이, so people born then are 검은 호랑이띠 — literally a black tiger, from the Water colour of the Water stem.
