1954 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Wood Horse

1954 is the Year of the Wood Horse (甲午, Jiǎ Wǔ), but only from Lunar New Year on Feb 3, 1954. A baby born on or after that date and up to Jan 23, 1955 is a Horse; anyone born from January 1 to Feb 2, 1954 is a Yin Water Snake (1953).

When did the 1954 Year of the Horse start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1954 that date was February 3, 1954.

Chinese Lunar New Year (start of the Horse year)Feb 3, 1954
Last day of the Horse yearJan 23, 1955
Korean Seollal (설날)Feb 4, 1954 differs from China
Vietnamese TếtFeb 3, 1954 (same day)
Ipchun / Lichun 立春 (Saju & Bazi year change)Feb 4, 1954, 17:30 Korea time (Feb 4 16:30 Beijing)
Japan (eto 干支 by calendar year)Jan 1 – Dec 31, 1954

Because Lunar New Year (Feb 3) came before Ipchun (Feb 4) in 1954, babies born from Feb 3 to Feb 3 are Horses by the popular zodiac but still have a 癸巳 (Water Snake) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Korea’s calendar is calculated for Korea time (UTC+9), one hour ahead of Beijing. In 1954 the new moon fell at 23:55 Beijing time, which was already the next day in Korea, so Seollal was Feb 4, 1954 while China celebrated on Feb 3, 1954. Korean almanacs from the 1940s–50s may not always match modern computation; check a family record if a birthday sits on this exact day.

Wood Horse meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1954 is stem 甲 (Gap / Jiǎ, Yang Wood) on branch 午 (O, the Horse), number 31 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Wood Horse” rather than just “Horse”.

Reading the element. Wood is the element of spring and growth. Wood years are traditionally linked with generosity, cooperation, a strong sense of fairness and a drive to improve things step by step. The flip side is that Wood people can spread themselves thin, start more projects than they finish, or become stubborn when their ideals are questioned. For a Wood year the keywords are growth, kindness and idealism. The Horse itself carries Fire as its fixed element, so this year blends wood energy with fire qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1954 and 1955 are Gold in the Sand (沙中金), pictured as gold mixed into sand: talent that needs sifting and polishing before others recognise its worth.

The same 甲午 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gap-O day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1954 is called the year of the 푸른 말띠 (blue/green horse). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1954 counts as uma (午, Horse) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1954 Wood Horse personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: free-spirited, energetic, sociable, direct + growth, kindness and idealism

Horse years are associated with energy, independence and enthusiasm. Horses are said to love movement and new experiences, speak their minds and win friends easily.

The horse stands at due south and at midday, the peak of yang energy. Fire Horse years carry special folklore in Japan (hinoeuma): births in Japan fell by about a quarter in 1966 because of the superstition, according to national vital statistics.

Where the Wood element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Wood Horse is cooperative, principled, keeps growing. Compared with the Water Horse of 1942 and the Fire Horse of 1966, the 1954 version is read as the most growth-minded and cooperative Horse.

FAQ: 1954 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1954?

1954 is the Year of the Yang Wood Horse (甲午, Jiǎ Wǔ). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 3, 1954 and ended on Jan 23, 1955, so people born in that window are Horses. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1954. Am I a Horse?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 3, 1954. Births from January 1 to Feb 2, 1954 belong to the Yin Water Snake of 1953. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1954 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1954 Horse?

Traditionally the Goat is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Tiger and Dog (Three Harmony). The Rat is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Ox forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1954 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1954 turns 72 during 2026 and 73 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1954 Wood Horse?

Six Harmony with the Goat. Your birth-year element, Wood, generates Fire. Tradition reads this as an output year: ideas, creativity and giving to others flow easily, but energy can drain, so rest and boundaries matter. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1954 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1954 is 갑오-nyeon (갑오년, Gap-O), the year of the 말, so people born then are 푸른 말띠 — literally a blue/green horse, from the Wood colour of the Wood stem.
