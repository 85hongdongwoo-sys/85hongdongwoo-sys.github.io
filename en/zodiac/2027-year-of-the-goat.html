Ipchun / Lichun 立春Feb 4, 2027 · 10:46 KST (09:46 Beijing)
Goat year ends (China)Jan 25, 2028 — Earth Monkey starts Jan 26, 2028 (Korea Jan 27)

The new moon that opens the year occurs at about 23:56 Beijing time on Feb 6, 2027 — four minutes before midnight. In Korea (UTC+9) it is already 00:56 on Feb 7, so the Korean lunar calendar starts the year a day later. This happens a few times a century (1997 and 2028 are other examples). Saju and Bazi ignore Lunar New Year for the year pillar and switch at Ipchun, the solar term when the sun reaches 315° longitude.

What does the Fire Goat year mean?

The stem 丁 (Jeong/Ding) is Yin Fire: the candle or lamp rather than the sun, a focused and warm light. The branch 未 (Mi/Wei) is the Goat, an Earth sign that stores the last heat of summer. Fire feeds Earth, so the pair is read as harmonious and nurturing — a year associated with care, culture, the arts, food, home and patient work rather than dramatic conquest. Its nayin is Heavenly River Water (天河水): rain from the Milky Way, the heavenly river: generosity, idealism and a nourishing influence that falls on everyone.

Fire Goat years in living memory were 1967 (and 1907). Each Goat year has its own flavour: Metal 1991, Water 2003, Wood 2015, Fire 2027, Earth 2039.

Tai Sui and samjae in 2027

Tai Sui (太歲) is the deity or star said to govern each year in Chinese folk religion. Signs that “offend Tai Sui” in 2027 are the Goat (值太歲, own year), the Ox (冲太歲, clash), the Rat (害太歲, harm) and the Dog (破/刑太歲, break and punishment). Temples in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Malaysia offer “安太歲” blessings around Lunar New Year.

Samjae (삼재, three calamities) is a Korean folk cycle based on the trines. The Pig–Rabbit–Goat group entered samjae in 2025 (Snake year), sat in its middle in 2026 (Horse year) and leaves it in 2027 (Goat year), the nal-samjae. It is widely known, mostly treated as light-hearted caution, and has no scientific basis.

FAQ: 2027 Year of the Goat

When does the Year of the Goat 2027 start?

In the Chinese calendar the Fire Goat year starts on Saturday, February 6, 2027 and ends on January 25, 2028. Korea celebrates Seollal on February 7, 2027. In Saju and Bazi the year pillar changes at Ipchun on February 4, 2027 (about 10:46 Korea time).

What element is 2027?

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未): stem 丁 is Yin Fire and the branch 未 is the Goat, an Earth sign. Its nayin is Heavenly River Water (天河水).

Which signs offend Tai Sui in 2027?

Traditional lists name the Goat (own year), Ox (clash and punishment), Rat (harm) and Dog (punishment and break).

Which signs are lucky in 2027?

By tradition the Horse (Six Harmony with the Goat) and the Rabbit and Pig (Wood trine with the Goat) are most supported. In Korean folk practice, Pig, Rabbit and Goat are also in the last year of samjae.

Is 2027 the year of the goat, sheep or ram?

All three: the character 未 covers goat, sheep and ram. Korean uses 양 (sheep), Vietnamese Dê (goat) and Japanese hitsuji (sheep).

What were the previous Fire Goat years?

1907 and 1967. The next Fire Goat year after 2027 is 2087.
