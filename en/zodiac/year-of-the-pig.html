Pig personality and symbolism

Pig years are traditionally read as kind, honest and generous. Pigs are said to enjoy life’s pleasures, work hard for the people they love and forgive easily.

Folk readings make the Pig the zodiac’s good-hearted host: trusting, cheerful and tolerant, happiest when everyone at the table is well fed.

The Pig closes the cycle and symbolises wealth and abundance; in Korea dreaming of a pig is a classic sign of luck. In Japan the twelfth sign is the wild boar (inoshishi) rather than the domestic pig.

Strengths (traditional)

  • generous
  • sincere
  • tolerant
  • diligent
  • optimistic

Cautions (traditional)

  • too trusting
  • indulgent
  • avoids conflict
  • can be naive with money

Love and relationships

Affectionate and devoted, putting effort into a comfortable home; Pigs want a sincere partner and dislike game-playing.

Career and work style

Suited to hospitality, food, healthcare, charity, education, the arts and roles built on trust.

These are traditional folk associations, not psychological findings.

The five types of Pig: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so there are five kinds of Pig that repeat every 60 years. The Pig’s own branch element is Water, so the Water Pig is the “purest” form of the sign.

2027 forecast for the Pig (Year of the Fire Goat)

2027 is the Yin Fire Goat year Jeongmi (丁未), from Feb 6, 2027 to Jan 25, 2028 in the Chinese calendar (Korean Seollal: Feb 7). For the Pig: Three Harmony (Wood trine) with the Goat.

Your sign shares the Wood trine (Pig–Rabbit–Goat) with 2027, a traditionally co-operative pairing that favours growth, learning and new networks. In Korean folk practice, though, Pig, Rabbit and Goat are in their final samjae (삼재) year, nal-samjae, so the advice is to finish things cleanly rather than overreach.

FAQ: Year of the Pig

What years are the Year of the Pig?

Recent Pig years are 1947, 1959, 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019, 2031. Each starts at Lunar New Year, not on January 1, so check the exact dates in the table.

Who should a Pig marry, according to tradition?

Tradition favours the Tiger (Six Harmony) and the Rabbit and Goat (Three Harmony). The Snake is the clashing sign. This is folklore for entertainment; real relationships depend on the people.

What are the five types of Pig?

Depending on the year’s stem, a Pig is Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water: Water Pig 1983; Wood Pig 1995; Fire Pig 1947, 2007; Earth Pig 1959, 2019; Metal Pig 1971, 2031.

Is 2027 a good year for the Pig?

Sources and method

  • Lunar New Year dates, new-moon instants and Ipchun (立春) times computed with the open-source lunar-javascript calendar library (Chinese calendar, Beijing time, UTC+8), bundled on this site. Korean Seollal differs from the Chinese date when the new moon falls just before midnight Beijing time; Korean dates here are derived for Korea Standard Time (e.g. Seollal 2027 is Feb 7 in Korea, Feb 6 in China — matching the published Korean public-holiday calendar).
  • Vietnamese calendar differences (1968 split between North and South, 1985 a month apart, Cat instead of Rabbit, Water Buffalo for Ox): Wikipedia, “Vietnamese calendar”.
  • Six Harmony, Three Harmony, Clash, Harm and Punishment groupings and the 60 nayin (納音) names: standard tables of the traditional Chinese calendar, also used by the site’s Saju calculator.
  • Lucky numbers and colours: as commonly listed in modern popular zodiac guides (e.g. eastchinatrip.com); lists vary between sources and are folklore, not tradition with a single authority.
  • Korea abolished “Korean age” for legal and administrative use on June 28, 2023 (amended Civil Act), so ages here are international ages unless labelled.
