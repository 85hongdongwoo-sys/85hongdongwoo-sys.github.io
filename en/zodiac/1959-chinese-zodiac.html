1959 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Earth Pig

If you were born between Feb 8, 1959 and Jan 27, 1960, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Pig — more precisely the Yin Earth Pig (己亥). Births earlier in 1959, from January 1 to Feb 7, 1959, still belong to the previous sign, the Yang Earth Dog (1958).

When did the 1959 Year of the Pig start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1959 that date was February 8, 1959.

In 1959 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 8), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 7 have a 己亥 (Earth Pig) year pillar in Saju but are still Dogs in the popular zodiac.

Earth Pig meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1959 is stem 己 (Gi / Jǐ, Yin Earth) on branch 亥 (Hae, the Pig), number 36 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Earth Pig” rather than just “Pig”.

Reading the element. Earth is the element of the centre and of harvest. Earth years are read as patient, realistic and dependable, with a talent for mediating and keeping a group together. Tradition notes a tendency to worry, to resist change and to keep feelings to oneself for too long. For a Earth year the keywords are stability, practicality and trust. The Pig itself carries Water as its fixed element, so this year blends earth energy with water qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1958 and 1959 are Flatland Wood (平地木), pictured as trees growing on open flatland: approachability, practical growth and a dislike of pretence.

The same 己亥 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gi-Hae day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1959 is called the year of the 황금 돼지띠 (yellow (gold) pig). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1959 counts as i (亥, Pig) regardless of the lunar date. In Japan the twelfth animal is the wild boar (inoshishi), not the domestic pig.

1959 Earth Pig personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: generous, warm-hearted, sincere, easygoing + stability, practicality and trust

Folk readings make the Pig the zodiac’s good-hearted host: trusting, cheerful and tolerant, happiest when everyone at the table is well fed.

The Pig closes the cycle and symbolises wealth and abundance; in Korea dreaming of a pig is a classic sign of luck. In Japan the twelfth sign is the wild boar (inoshishi) rather than the domestic pig.

Where the Earth element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Earth Pig is reliable, patient, grounded. Compared with the Fire Pig of 1947 and the Metal Pig of 1971, the 1959 version is read as the steadiest and most practical Pig.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1959

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a supportive year for the Earth Pig:

Your sign shares the Wood trine (Pig–Rabbit–Goat) with 2027, a traditionally co-operative pairing that favours growth, learning and new networks. In Korean folk practice, though, Pig, Rabbit and Goat are in their final samjae (삼재) year, nal-samjae, so the advice is to finish things cleanly rather than overreach.

Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar.

FAQ: 1959 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1959?

1959 is the Year of the Yin Earth Pig (己亥, Jǐ Hài). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 8, 1959 and ended on Jan 27, 1960, so people born in that window are Pigs. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1959. Am I a Pig?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 8, 1959. Births from January 1 to Feb 7, 1959 belong to the Yang Earth Dog of 1958. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1959 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1959 Pig?

Traditionally the Tiger is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rabbit and Goat (Three Harmony). The Snake is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Monkey forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1959 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1959 turns 67 during 2026 and 68 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1959 Earth Pig?

Three Harmony (Wood trine) with the Goat. Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1959 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1959 is 기해-nyeon (기해년, Gi-Hae), the year of the 돼지, so people born then are 황금 돼지띠 — literally a yellow (gold) pig, from the Earth colour of the Earth stem.
