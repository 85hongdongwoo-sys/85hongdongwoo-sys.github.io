Snake personality and symbolism

Snake years are traditionally read as thoughtful, perceptive and self-controlled. Snakes are said to plan quietly, read people well and move decisively once the moment is right.

In folk readings the Snake is the strategist of the zodiac: elegant, reserved and deeply analytical, with a sixth sense for hidden motives.

Snakes were linked with wisdom, healing and the guarding of treasure. In Korean homes a house snake (eopgureongi) was once believed to protect the family’s wealth, so the sign is not as sinister as Western imagery suggests.

Love and relationships

Intense and devoted, but private; Snakes value loyalty very highly and can be possessive if trust is shaken.

Career and work style

Suited to research, analysis, finance, psychology, law, medicine, investigation and strategy roles.

These are traditional folk associations, not psychological findings.

2027 forecast for the Snake (Year of the Fire Goat)

2027 is the Yin Fire Goat year Jeongmi (丁未), from Feb 6, 2027 to Jan 25, 2028 in the Chinese calendar (Korean Seollal: Feb 7). For the Snake: Seasonal (directional) Fire group with the Goat.

Snake, Horse and Goat together make the summer directional group (巳午未), so the Snake meets a familiar, warm Fire-Earth energy in 2027. Tradition sees this as a year to push visible projects, while minding over-heating schedules.

FAQ: Year of the Snake

What years are the Year of the Snake?

Recent Snake years are 1941, 1953, 1965, 1977, 1989, 2001, 2013, 2025. Each starts at Lunar New Year, not on January 1, so check the exact dates in the table.

Who should a Snake marry, according to tradition?

Tradition favours the Monkey (Six Harmony) and the Rooster and Ox (Three Harmony). The Pig is the clashing sign. This is folklore for entertainment; real relationships depend on the people.

What are the five types of Snake?

Depending on the year’s stem, a Snake is Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water: Fire Snake 1977; Earth Snake 1989; Metal Snake 1941, 2001; Water Snake 1953, 2013; Wood Snake 1965, 2025.

Is 2027 a good year for the Snake?

Seasonal (directional) Fire group with the Goat: Snake, Horse and Goat together make the summer directional group (巳午未), so the Snake meets a familiar, warm Fire-Earth energy in 2027. Tradition sees this as a year to push visible projects, while minding over-heating schedules.

What is the Snake in Korean?

The Snake is 뱀 (巳) in Korean, and people born in its years are called 뱀띠.
