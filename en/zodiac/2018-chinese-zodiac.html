When did the 2018 Year of the Dog start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 2018 that date was February 16, 2018.

In 2018 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 16), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 15 have a 戊戌 (Earth Dog) year pillar in Saju but are still Roosters in the popular zodiac.

Earth Dog meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 2018 is stem 戊 (Mu / Wù, Yang Earth) on branch 戌 (Sul, the Dog), number 35 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Earth Dog” rather than just “Dog”.

Reading the element. Earth is the element of the centre and of harvest. Earth years are read as patient, realistic and dependable, with a talent for mediating and keeping a group together. Tradition notes a tendency to worry, to resist change and to keep feelings to oneself for too long. For a Earth year the keywords are stability, practicality and trust. The Dog itself carries Earth as its fixed element, so this year blends earth energy twice over — a “pure” Earth year with its branch.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 2018 and 2019 are Flatland Wood (平地木), pictured as trees growing on open flatland: approachability, practical growth and a dislike of pretence.

The same 戊戌 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Mu-Sul day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 2018 is called the year of the 황금 개띠 (yellow (gold) dog). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 2018 counts as inu (戌, Dog) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

2018 Earth Dog personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: loyal, honest, protective, fair + stability, practicality and trust

Dog years are associated with loyalty, a strong sense of justice and sincerity. Dogs are said to be dependable friends who defend the vulnerable and speak up when something is unfair.

As guardian of the home, the dog stands for loyalty and justice. In Japan the Dog is linked with easy childbirth, so expectant mothers traditionally visit shrines on a Dog day of the calendar.

Where the Earth element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Earth Dog is reliable, patient, grounded. Compared with the Fire Dog of 2006 and the Metal Dog of 2030, the 2018 version is read as the steadiest and most practical Dog.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 2018

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a year to move carefully for the Earth Dog:

Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar.

FAQ: 2018 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 2018?

2018 is the Year of the Yang Earth Dog (戊戌, Wù Xū). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 16, 2018 and ended on Feb 4, 2019, so people born in that window are Dogs. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 2018. Am I a Dog?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 16, 2018. Births from January 1 to Feb 15, 2018 belong to the Yin Fire Rooster of 2017. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 2018 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 2018 Dog?

Traditionally the Rabbit is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Tiger and Horse (Three Harmony). The Dragon is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Rooster forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 2018 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 2018 turns 8 during 2026 and 9 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 2018 Earth Dog?

Punishment / break with Tai Sui (刑·破太岁). Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 2018 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 2018 is 무술-nyeon (무술년, Mu-Sul), the year of the 개, so people born then are 황금 개띠 — literally a yellow (gold) dog, from the Earth colour of the Earth stem.
