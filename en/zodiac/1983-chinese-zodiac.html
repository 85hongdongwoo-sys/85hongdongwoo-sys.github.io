1983 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Water Pig

If you were born between Feb 13, 1983 and Feb 1, 1984, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Pig — more precisely the Yin Water Pig (癸亥). Births earlier in 1983, from January 1 to Feb 12, 1983, still belong to the previous sign, the Yang Water Dog (1982).

When did the 1983 Year of the Pig start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1983 that date was February 13, 1983.

In 1983 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 13), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 12 have a 癸亥 (Water Pig) year pillar in Saju but are still Dogs in the popular zodiac.

Water Pig meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1983 is stem 癸 (Gye / Guǐ, Yin Water) on branch 亥 (Hae, the Pig), number 60 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Water Pig” rather than just “Pig”.

Reading the element. Water is the element of winter, depth and flow. Water years are associated with intelligence, intuition, diplomacy and the ability to adapt to any container. Tradition warns of over-flexibility: Water can drift, keep options open too long or hide its real feelings. For a Water year the keywords are wisdom, adaptability and communication. The Pig itself carries Water as its fixed element, so this year blends water energy twice over — a “pure” Water year with its branch.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1982 and 1983 are Great Sea Water (大海水), pictured as the great sea: depth, tolerance and a capacity to take in very different people and ideas.

The same 癸亥 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gye-Hae day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1983 is called the year of the 검은 돼지띠 (black pig). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1983 counts as i (亥, Pig) regardless of the lunar date. In Japan the twelfth animal is the wild boar (inoshishi), not the domestic pig.

1983 Water Pig personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: generous, warm-hearted, sincere, easygoing + wisdom, adaptability and communication

Folk readings make the Pig the zodiac’s good-hearted host: trusting, cheerful and tolerant, happiest when everyone at the table is well fed.

The Pig closes the cycle and symbolises wealth and abundance; in Korea dreaming of a pig is a classic sign of luck. In Japan the twelfth sign is the wild boar (inoshishi) rather than the domestic pig.

Where the Water element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Water Pig is perceptive, diplomatic, adaptable. Compared with the Metal Pig of 1971 and the Wood Pig of 1995, the 1983 version is read as the most adaptable and diplomatic Pig.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1983

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a supportive year for the Water Pig:

Your sign shares the Wood trine (Pig–Rabbit–Goat) with 2027, a traditionally co-operative pairing that favours growth, learning and new networks. In Korean folk practice, though, Pig, Rabbit and Goat are in their final samjae (삼재) year, nal-samjae, so the advice is to finish things cleanly rather than overreach.

Water puts out Fire, so your element is the one in control. In traditional terms the year becomes a "wealth" year: opportunities you have to chase and manage, with gains that reflect the work invested.

FAQ: 1983 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1983?

1983 is the Year of the Yin Water Pig (癸亥, Guǐ Hài). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 13, 1983 and ended on Feb 1, 1984, so people born in that window are Pigs. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1983. Am I a Pig?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 13, 1983. Births from January 1 to Feb 12, 1983 belong to the Yang Water Dog of 1982. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1983 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1983 Pig?

Traditionally the Tiger is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rabbit and Goat (Three Harmony). The Snake is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Monkey forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1983 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1983 turns 43 during 2026 and 44 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1983 Water Pig?

Three Harmony (Wood trine) with the Goat. Water puts out Fire, so your element is the one in control. In traditional terms the year becomes a "wealth" year: opportunities you have to chase and manage, with gains that reflect the work invested. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1983 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1983 is 계해-nyeon (계해년, Gye-Hae), the year of the 돼지, so people born then are 검은 돼지띠 — literally a black pig, from the Water colour of the Water stem.
