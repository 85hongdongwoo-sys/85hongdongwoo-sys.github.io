When did the 1979 Year of the Goat start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1979 that date was January 28, 1979.

Because Lunar New Year (Jan 28) came before Ipchun (Feb 4) in 1979, babies born from Jan 28 to Feb 3 are Goats by the popular zodiac but still have a 戊午 (Earth Horse) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Earth Goat meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1979 is stem 己 (Gi / Jǐ, Yin Earth) on branch 未 (Mi, the Goat), number 56 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Earth Goat” rather than just “Goat”.

Reading the element. Earth is the element of the centre and of harvest. Earth years are read as patient, realistic and dependable, with a talent for mediating and keeping a group together. Tradition notes a tendency to worry, to resist change and to keep feelings to oneself for too long. For a Earth year the keywords are stability, practicality and trust. The Goat itself carries Earth as its fixed element, so this year blends earth energy twice over — a “pure” Earth year with its branch.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1978 and 1979 are Sky Fire (天上火), pictured as fire in the sky, the sun itself: radiance, optimism and a public presence that warms many people at once.

The same 己未 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Gi-Mi day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1979 is called the year of the 황금 양띠 (yellow (gold) goat). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1979 counts as hitsuji (未, Goat) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1979 Earth Goat personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: gentle, creative, compassionate, calm + stability, practicality and trust

Goat years are traditionally read as kind, artistic and peace-loving. Goats are said to be sensitive to beauty and to other people’s feelings, and to do their best work in a calm, supportive setting.

The character 未 is translated as Goat, Sheep or Ram; Korean uses 양 (yang, sheep/goat), Vietnamese uses Dê (goat) and Japanese hitsuji (sheep). In Chinese the word for sheep sounds like the word for yang energy, so the animal is linked with good fortune and peace.

Where the Earth element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Earth Goat is reliable, patient, grounded. Compared with the Fire Goat of 1967 and the Metal Goat of 1991, the 1979 version is read as the steadiest and most practical Goat.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1979

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a high-visibility year for the Earth Goat:

In Chinese folk belief your own sign’s year is a "Tai Sui" year in which you sit directly under the year’s ruling star. Rather than bad luck, tradition reads it as a year of visibility and change that rewards careful planning; wearing red is the popular charm. Goat, Rabbit and Pig are also in the last year of the Korean samjae (삼재) cycle in 2027, called nal-samjae (날삼재), the "leaving" year.

Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar.

FAQ: 1979 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1979?

1979 is the Year of the Yin Earth Goat (己未, Jǐ Wèi). The year began at Lunar New Year on Jan 28, 1979 and ended on Feb 15, 1980, so people born in that window are Goats. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1979. Am I a Goat?

Only if you were born on or after Jan 28, 1979. Births from January 1 to Jan 27, 1979 belong to the Yang Earth Horse of 1978. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1979 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1979 Goat?

Traditionally the Horse is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Pig and Rabbit (Three Harmony). The Ox is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Rat forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1979 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1979 turns 47 during 2026 and 48 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1979 Earth Goat?

Own sign year (本命年, benmingnian). Fire generates Earth, so for an Earth-year sign the Fire Goat year is traditionally supportive: help from mentors, study, documents and recognition. The Goat itself is also an Earth branch, a double dose of the familiar. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1979 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1979 is 기미-nyeon (기미년, Gi-Mi), the year of the 양, so people born then are 황금 양띠 — literally a yellow (gold) goat, from the Earth colour of the Earth stem.
