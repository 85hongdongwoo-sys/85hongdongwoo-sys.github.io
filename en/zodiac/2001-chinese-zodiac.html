If you were born between Jan 24, 2001 and Feb 11, 2002, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Snake — more precisely the Yin Metal Snake (辛巳). Births earlier in 2001, from January 1 to Jan 23, 2001, still belong to the previous sign, the Yang Metal Dragon (2000).

When did the 2001 Year of the Snake start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 2001 that date was January 24, 2001.

Because Lunar New Year (Jan 24) came before Ipchun (Feb 4) in 2001, babies born from Jan 24 to Feb 3 are Snakes by the popular zodiac but still have a 庚辰 (Metal Dragon) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Metal Snake meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 2001 is stem 辛 (Sin / Xīn, Yin Metal) on branch 巳 (Sa, the Snake), number 18 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Metal Snake” rather than just “Snake”.

Reading the element. Metal is the element of autumn and of cutting away what is unnecessary. Metal years are linked with willpower, self-discipline, loyalty and a strong sense of right and wrong. The traditional caution is rigidity: Metal can seem cool, blunt or unwilling to compromise. For a Metal year the keywords are determination, principle and clarity. The Snake itself carries Fire as its fixed element, so this year blends metal energy with fire qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 2000 and 2001 are White Wax Metal (白蠟金), pictured as metal still soft like wax, not yet fully hardened: potential that is shaped by education and good company.

The same 辛巳 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Sin-Sa day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 2001 is called the year of the 흰 뱀띠 (white snake). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 2001 counts as mi (巳, Snake) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

2001 Metal Snake personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: wise, intuitive, calm, private + determination, principle and clarity

Snake years are traditionally read as thoughtful, perceptive and self-controlled. Snakes are said to plan quietly, read people well and move decisively once the moment is right.

Snakes were linked with wisdom, healing and the guarding of treasure. In Korean homes a house snake (eopgureongi) was once believed to protect the family’s wealth, so the sign is not as sinister as Western imagery suggests.

Where the Metal element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Metal Snake is disciplined, loyal, decisive. Compared with the Earth Snake of 1989 and the Water Snake of 2013, the 2001 version is read as the most determined and principled Snake.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 2001

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a supportive year for the Metal Snake:

FAQ: 2001 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 2001?

2001 is the Year of the Yin Metal Snake (辛巳, Xīn Sì). The year began at Lunar New Year on Jan 24, 2001 and ended on Feb 11, 2002, so people born in that window are Snakes. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 2001. Am I a Snake?

Only if you were born on or after Jan 24, 2001. Births from January 1 to Jan 23, 2001 belong to the Yang Metal Dragon of 2000. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 2001 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 2001 Snake?

Traditionally the Monkey is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rooster and Ox (Three Harmony). The Pig is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Tiger forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 2001 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 2001 turns 25 during 2026 and 26 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 2001 Metal Snake?

Seasonal (directional) Fire group with the Goat. Fire melts Metal, so the year’s energy "controls" your element. Tradition reads it as a year of pressure and responsibility that can bring promotion or new titles if you stay disciplined and pace yourself. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 2001 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 2001 is 신사-nyeon (신사년, Sin-Sa), the year of the 뱀, so people born then are 흰 뱀띠 — literally a white snake, from the Metal colour of the Metal stem.
