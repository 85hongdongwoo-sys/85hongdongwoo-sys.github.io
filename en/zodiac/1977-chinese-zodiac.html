1977 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Fire Snake

If you were born between Feb 18, 1977 and Feb 6, 1978, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Snake — more precisely the Yin Fire Snake (丁巳). Births earlier in 1977, from January 1 to Feb 17, 1977, still belong to the previous sign, the Yang Fire Dragon (1976).

When did the 1977 Year of the Snake start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1977 that date was February 18, 1977.

In 1977 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 18), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 17 have a 丁巳 (Fire Snake) year pillar in Saju but are still Dragons in the popular zodiac.

Fire Snake meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1977 is stem 丁 (Jeong / Dīng, Yin Fire) on branch 巳 (Sa, the Snake), number 54 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Fire Snake” rather than just “Snake”.

Reading the element. Fire is the element of summer and visibility. Fire years are associated with enthusiasm, charisma, quick decisions and a need to share feelings openly. Tradition warns that the same heat can flare into impatience or burn out when a project stops being exciting. For a Fire year the keywords are passion, warmth and expression. The Snake itself carries Fire as its fixed element, so this year blends fire energy twice over — a “pure” Fire year with its branch.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1976 and 1977 are Sandy Earth (沙中土), pictured as earth within sand: adaptability, a light touch and a knack for settling into new environments.

The same 丁巳 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Jeong-Sa day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1977 is called the year of the 붉은 뱀띠 (red snake). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1977 counts as mi (巳, Snake) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1977 Fire Snake personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: wise, intuitive, calm, private + passion, warmth and expression

Snake years are traditionally read as thoughtful, perceptive and self-controlled. Snakes are said to plan quietly, read people well and move decisively once the moment is right.

Snakes were linked with wisdom, healing and the guarding of treasure. In Korean homes a house snake (eopgureongi) was once believed to protect the family’s wealth, so the sign is not as sinister as Western imagery suggests.

Where the Fire element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Fire Snake is enthusiastic, expressive, inspiring. Compared with the Wood Snake of 1965 and the Earth Snake of 1989, the 1977 version is read as the most expressive and fiery Snake.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1977

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. FAQ: 1977 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1977?

1977 is the Year of the Yin Fire Snake (丁巳, Dīng Sì). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 18, 1977 and ended on Feb 6, 1978, so people born in that window are Snakes. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1977. Am I a Snake?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 18, 1977. Births from January 1 to Feb 17, 1977 belong to the Yang Fire Dragon of 1976. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1977 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1977 Snake?

Traditionally the Monkey is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rooster and Ox (Three Harmony). The Pig is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Tiger forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1977 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1977 turns 49 during 2026 and 50 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1977 Fire Snake?

Seasonal (directional) Fire group with the Goat. Your birth-year element is the same as the year’s stem. Expect a confidence boost and more peers, competitors and social invitations; tradition warns against impulsive spending and ego clashes. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1977 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1977 is 정사-nyeon (정사년, Jeong-Sa), the year of the 뱀, so people born then are 붉은 뱀띠 — literally a red snake, from the Fire colour of the Fire stem.
