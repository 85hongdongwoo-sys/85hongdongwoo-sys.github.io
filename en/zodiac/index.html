How the 60-year cycle works

Each year combines a heavenly stem (which sets the element and yin-yang) with an earthly branch (the animal). Ten stems and twelve branches line up every 60 years, so 1990 and 2050 are both Metal Horses. The last digit of a year tells you the element: 0–1 Metal, 2–3 Water, 4–5 Wood, 6–7 Fire, 8–9 Earth.

FAQ: Chinese zodiac by year

What is my Chinese zodiac sign?

Find your birth year in the table. If you were born in January or February, compare your birthday with that year’s Lunar New Year date: before it, you belong to the previous animal.

What is the order of the 12 zodiac animals?

Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig.

What is the current Chinese zodiac year?

From Feb 17, 2026 to Feb 5, 2027 it is the Year of the Fire Horse (丙午). The Fire Goat (丁未) begins on Feb 6, 2027.

What is the difference between the Chinese zodiac and Korean ddi?

They use the same 12 animals and 60-year cycle. Korean ddi follows the Korean lunar calendar (Seollal), which can be one day later than China’s; Korean Saju uses Ipchun (around Feb 4) for the year pillar.

How is the element of a year found?

From the last digit of the year: 0–1 Metal, 2–3 Water, 4–5 Wood, 6–7 Fire, 8–9 Earth; even years are yang and odd years yin.

Sources and method

  • Lunar New Year dates, new-moon instants and Ipchun (立春) times computed with the open-source lunar-javascript calendar library (Chinese calendar, Beijing time, UTC+8), bundled on this site. Korean Seollal differs from the Chinese date when the new moon falls just before midnight Beijing time; Korean dates here are derived for Korea Standard Time (e.g. Seollal 2027 is Feb 7 in Korea, Feb 6 in China — matching the published Korean public-holiday calendar).
  • Vietnamese calendar differences (1968 split between North and South, 1985 a month apart, Cat instead of Rabbit, Water Buffalo for Ox): Wikipedia, “Vietnamese calendar”.
  • Six Harmony, Three Harmony, Clash, Harm and Punishment groupings and the 60 nayin (納音) names: standard tables of the traditional Chinese calendar, also used by the site’s Saju calculator.
  • Lucky numbers and colours: as commonly listed in modern popular zodiac guides (e.g. eastchinatrip.com); lists vary between sources and are folklore, not tradition with a single authority.
  • Korea abolished “Korean age” for legal and administrative use on June 28, 2023 (amended Civil Act), so ages here are international ages unless labelled.
