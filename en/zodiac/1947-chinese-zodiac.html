1947 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Fire Pig

If you were born between Jan 22, 1947 and Feb 9, 1948, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Pig — more precisely the Yin Fire Pig (丁亥). Births earlier in 1947, from January 1 to Jan 21, 1947, still belong to the previous sign, the Yang Fire Dog (1946).

When did the 1947 Year of the Pig start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1947 that date was January 22, 1947.

Because Lunar New Year (Jan 22) came before Ipchun (Feb 5) in 1947, babies born from Jan 22 to Feb 4 are Pigs by the popular zodiac but still have a 丙戌 (Fire Dog) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Fire Pig meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1947 is stem 丁 (Jeong / Dīng, Yin Fire) on branch 亥 (Hae, the Pig), number 24 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Fire Pig” rather than just “Pig”.

Reading the element. Fire is the element of summer and visibility. Fire years are associated with enthusiasm, charisma, quick decisions and a need to share feelings openly. Tradition warns that the same heat can flare into impatience or burn out when a project stops being exciting. For a Fire year the keywords are passion, warmth and expression. The Pig itself carries Water as its fixed element, so this year blends fire energy with water qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1946 and 1947 are Rooftop Earth (屋上土), pictured as the earth tiles of a roof: shelter, responsibility for a household and steady, unglamorous reliability.

The same 丁亥 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Jeong-Hae day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1947 is called the year of the 붉은 돼지띠 (red pig). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1947 counts as i (亥, Pig) regardless of the lunar date. In Japan the twelfth animal is the wild boar (inoshishi), not the domestic pig.

1947 Fire Pig personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: generous, warm-hearted, sincere, easygoing + passion, warmth and expression

Pig years are traditionally read as kind, honest and generous. Pigs are said to enjoy life’s pleasures, work hard for the people they love and forgive easily.

The Pig closes the cycle and symbolises wealth and abundance; in Korea dreaming of a pig is a classic sign of luck. In Japan the twelfth sign is the wild boar (inoshishi) rather than the domestic pig.

Where the Fire element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Fire Pig is enthusiastic, expressive, inspiring. Compared with the Wood Pig of 1935 and the Earth Pig of 1959, the 1947 version is read as the most expressive and fiery Pig.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1947

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a supportive year for the Fire Pig:

FAQ: 1947 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1947?

1947 is the Year of the Yin Fire Pig (丁亥, Dīng Hài). The year began at Lunar New Year on Jan 22, 1947 and ended on Feb 9, 1948, so people born in that window are Pigs. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1947. Am I a Pig?

Only if you were born on or after Jan 22, 1947. Births from January 1 to Jan 21, 1947 belong to the Yang Fire Dog of 1946. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 5, 1947 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1947 Pig?

Traditionally the Tiger is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Rabbit and Goat (Three Harmony). The Snake is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Monkey forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1947 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1947 turns 79 during 2026 and 80 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1947 Fire Pig?

Three Harmony (Wood trine) with the Goat. Your birth-year element is the same as the year’s stem. Expect a confidence boost and more peers, competitors and social invitations; tradition warns against impulsive spending and ego clashes. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1947 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1947 is 정해-nyeon (정해년, Jeong-Hae), the year of the 돼지, so people born then are 붉은 돼지띠 — literally a red pig, from the Fire colour of the Fire stem.
