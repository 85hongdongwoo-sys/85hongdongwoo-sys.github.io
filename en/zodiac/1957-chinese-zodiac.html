When did the 1957 Year of the Rooster start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1957 that date was January 31, 1957.

Last day of the Rooster yearFeb 17, 1958

Because Lunar New Year (Jan 31) came before Ipchun (Feb 4) in 1957, babies born from Jan 31 to Feb 3 are Roosters by the popular zodiac but still have a 丙申 (Fire Monkey) year pillar in a Korean Saju or Chinese Bazi chart.

Fire Rooster meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1957 is stem 丁 (Jeong / Dīng, Yin Fire) on branch 酉 (Yu, the Rooster), number 34 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Fire Rooster” rather than just “Rooster”.

Reading the element. Fire is the element of summer and visibility. Fire years are associated with enthusiasm, charisma, quick decisions and a need to share feelings openly. Tradition warns that the same heat can flare into impatience or burn out when a project stops being exciting. For a Fire year the keywords are passion, warmth and expression. The Rooster itself carries Metal as its fixed element, so this year blends fire energy with metal qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1956 and 1957 are Fire below the Mountain (山下火), pictured as a fire burning at the foot of a mountain: warmth for those nearby and a light that is gentle rather than blazing.

The same 丁酉 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Jeong-Yu day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1957 is called the year of the 붉은 닭띠 (red rooster). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1957 counts as tori (酉, Rooster) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1957 Fire Rooster personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: observant, hard-working, confident, punctual + passion, warmth and expression

In folk readings the Rooster is the zodiac’s organiser: punctual, well-presented and hard-working, proud of doing things properly.

The rooster that crows at dawn was believed to chase away night spirits. Tradition credits the bird with five virtues: civility (its comb), martial spirit (spurs), courage, benevolence (sharing food) and trustworthiness (keeping time).

Where the Fire element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Fire Rooster is enthusiastic, expressive, inspiring. Compared with the Wood Rooster of 1945 and the Earth Rooster of 1969, the 1957 version is read as the most expressive and fiery Rooster.

In love: Loyal and attentive, with clear expectations; Roosters appreciate a partner who is honest and who notices their effort. At work: Suited to accounting, auditing, journalism, the military, management, quality control and any detail-heavy profession.

Zodiac personality descriptions are folklore. Real personality is shaped by upbringing, culture and choices, and people born in the same year differ enormously.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1957

FAQ: 1957 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1957?

1957 is the Year of the Yin Fire Rooster (丁酉, Dīng Yǒu). The year began at Lunar New Year on Jan 31, 1957 and ended on Feb 17, 1958, so people born in that window are Roosters. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1957. Am I a Rooster?

Only if you were born on or after Jan 31, 1957. Births from January 1 to Jan 30, 1957 belong to the Yang Fire Monkey of 1956. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1957 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1957 Rooster?

Traditionally the Dragon is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Snake and Ox (Three Harmony). The Rabbit is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Dog forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1957 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1957 turns 69 during 2026 and 70 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1957 Fire Rooster?

No special relationship with the Goat. Your birth-year element is the same as the year’s stem. Expect a confidence boost and more peers, competitors and social invitations; tradition warns against impulsive spending and ego clashes. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1957 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1957 is 정유-nyeon (정유년, Jeong-Yu), the year of the 닭, so people born then are 붉은 닭띠 — literally a red rooster, from the Fire colour of the Fire stem.
