When did the 1967 Year of the Goat start and end?

The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so each animal year starts at Lunar New Year, the second new moon after the winter solstice, which always lands between January 21 and February 20. For 1967 that date was February 9, 1967.

In 1967 Ipchun (Feb 4) came before Lunar New Year (Feb 9), so babies born from Feb 4 to Feb 8 have a 丁未 (Fire Goat) year pillar in Saju but are still Horses in the popular zodiac.

Fire Goat meaning: element, yin-yang and nayin

Every year in the 60-year cycle pairs one of ten heavenly stems with one of twelve earthly branches. 1967 is stem 丁 (Jeong / Dīng, Yin Fire) on branch 未 (Mi, the Goat), number 44 of 60 in the cycle. The stem gives the year its element, which is why people say “Fire Goat” rather than just “Goat”.

Reading the element. Fire is the element of summer and visibility. Fire years are associated with enthusiasm, charisma, quick decisions and a need to share feelings openly. Tradition warns that the same heat can flare into impatience or burn out when a project stops being exciting. For a Fire year the keywords are passion, warmth and expression. The Goat itself carries Earth as its fixed element, so this year blends fire energy with earth qualities.

The nayin image. The 60 pairs also have 30 poetic “nayin” names, shared by two neighbouring years. 1966 and 1967 are Heavenly River Water (天河水), pictured as rain from the Milky Way, the heavenly river: generosity, idealism and a nourishing influence that falls on everyone.

The same 丁未 pair is also a Saju day pillar; see the Jeong-Mi day-pillar article for how the combination is read when it appears on the day of birth.

Koreans often name the year with a colour taken from the stem element: Wood years are blue-green (청), Fire red (적), Earth yellow or golden (황), Metal white (백) and Water black (흑). That is why 1967 is called the year of the 붉은 양띠 (red goat). Japan has counted eto by the Gregorian year since 1873, so in Japan everyone born in 1967 counts as hitsuji (未, Goat) regardless of the lunar date. Vietnam uses the same animal for this year.

1967 Fire Goat personality traits (traditional)

Keywords: gentle, creative, compassionate, calm + passion, warmth and expression

Folk readings make the Goat the zodiac’s artist and caregiver: gentle, imaginative and generous, with a stubborn streak when its values are pushed.

The character 未 is translated as Goat, Sheep or Ram; Korean uses 양 (yang, sheep/goat), Vietnamese uses Dê (goat) and Japanese hitsuji (sheep). In Chinese the word for sheep sounds like the word for yang energy, so the animal is linked with good fortune and peace.

Where the Fire element shows. Tradition says the stem colours the animal: a Fire Goat is enthusiastic, expressive, inspiring. Compared with the Wood Goat of 1955 and the Earth Goat of 1979, the 1967 version is read as the most expressive and fiery Goat.

2027 Year of the Fire Goat: outlook for people born in 1967

2027 is Jeongmi (丁未), the Yin Fire Goat — called the “red sheep” year (붉은 양띠) in Korea. In the Chinese calendar it begins on Feb 6, 2027 and ends on Jan 25, 2028; Korea celebrates Seollal on Feb 7, 2027 because the new moon falls just after midnight Korea time. For Saju, the year pillar changes at Ipchun on Feb 4, 2027 at about 10:46 KST. Tradition reads 2027 as a high-visibility year for the Fire Goat:

In Chinese folk belief your own sign’s year is a "Tai Sui" year in which you sit directly under the year’s ruling star. Goat, Rabbit and Pig are also in the last year of the Korean samjae (삼재) cycle in 2027, called nal-samjae (날삼재), the "leaving" year.

FAQ: 1967 Chinese zodiac

What Chinese zodiac sign is 1967?

1967 is the Year of the Yin Fire Goat (丁未, Dīng Wèi). The year began at Lunar New Year on Feb 9, 1967 and ended on Jan 29, 1968, so people born in that window are Goats. This is cultural tradition, shared for entertainment.

I was born in January or February 1967. Am I a Goat?

Only if you were born on or after Feb 9, 1967. Births from January 1 to Feb 8, 1967 belong to the Yang Fire Horse of 1966. Korean Saju uses a different boundary, Ipchun on Feb 4, 1967 (Korea time), for the year pillar.

Who is the best match for a 1967 Goat?

Traditionally the Horse is the best match (Six Harmony), followed by the Pig and Rabbit (Three Harmony). The Ox is the opposite, clashing sign, and the Rat forms a Harm pair.

How old is someone born in 1967 in 2026 and 2027?

Someone born in 1967 turns 59 during 2026 and 60 during 2027 (international age; before the birthday they are a year younger). Korea’s traditional count age would add one year, but it has not been used officially since June 2023.

What does the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat mean for the 1967 Fire Goat?

Own sign year (本命年, benmingnian). Your birth-year element is the same as the year’s stem. Expect a confidence boost and more peers, competitors and social invitations; tradition warns against impulsive spending and ego clashes. Read it as cultural tradition for fun, not as a prediction.

What is 1967 in the Korean zodiac (ddi)?

In Korean, 1967 is 정미-nyeon (정미년, Jeong-Mi), the year of the 양, so people born then are 붉은 양띠 — literally a red goat, from the Fire colour of the Fire stem.
