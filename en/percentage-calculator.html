How it works

X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y. X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15% of 240?
36.
How do I calculate a percentage increase?
Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.
How do I work out a discount?
Price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). 20% off $50 is $40.
