The 12 Earthly Branches: Ja to Hae

The twelve earthly branches are the zodiac animals of Korean Saju, but they also carry seasons, hours and hidden stems. This guide covers each branch as a day branch (spouse palace) and shows which branches harmonise or clash, including with the 2027 Goat year.

The twelve branches at a glance

The twelve earthly branches (지지, jiji) are the bottom characters of every Saju pillar. They are the familiar zodiac animals, but in Saju they also mark months, two-hour periods and compass directions, and each one carries hidden stems. In your day pillar the branch is the spouse palace.

Three families: starting, cardinal and storage

Starting branches (In, Sa, Sin, Hae) open each season and are linked with movement (Yeokma). Cardinal branches (Ja, Myo, O, Yu) are the pure middle of each season and are linked with charm (Dohwa, peach blossom). Storage branches (Jin, Mi, Sul, Chuk) close each season and keep its energy, linked with reflection and art (Hwagae). These labels are traditional vocabulary, used on every day pillar page.

Each branch as a day branch

子 Ja — the Rat

Ja (子) is midwinter water at midnight: quiet on the surface, alive underneath. As a day branch it gives an inner world that is clever, resourceful and private. Cardinal (“peach-blossom”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Chuk · clash: O · day pillars: Gap-Ja, Byeong-Ja, Mu-Ja, Gyeong-Ja, Im-Ja.

丑 Chuk — the Ox

Chuk (丑) is frozen winter soil that stores water and metal. As a day branch it adds patience, frugality and a slow, sure way of building things. Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Ja · clash: Mi · day pillars: Eul-Chuk, Jeong-Chuk, Gi-Chuk, Sin-Chuk, Gye-Chuk.

寅 In — the Tiger

In (寅) is the first spring branch, a tiger in a waking forest. As a day branch it adds courage, initiative and a taste for new ground. Starting (“post-horse”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Hae · clash: Sin · day pillars: Byeong-In, Mu-In, Gyeong-In, Im-In, Gap-In.

卯 Myo — the Rabbit

Myo (卯) is pure spring wood, grass and blossoms. As a day branch it adds charm, sensitivity and a gentle but persistent will. Cardinal (“peach-blossom”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Sul · clash: Yu · day pillars: Jeong-Myo, Gi-Myo, Sin-Myo, Gye-Myo, Eul-Myo.

辰 Jin — the Dragon

Jin (辰) is damp spring earth that stores water, often linked with the dragon. As a day branch it adds ambition, imagination and a mix of practicality and big dreams. Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Yu · clash: Sul · day pillars: Mu-Jin, Gyeong-Jin, Im-Jin, Gap-Jin, Byeong-Jin.

巳 Sa — the Snake

Sa (巳) is early-summer fire with metal hidden inside. As a day branch it adds quick thinking, strategy and a warm but watchful presence. Starting (“post-horse”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Sin · clash: Hae · day pillars: Gi-Sa, Sin-Sa, Gye-Sa, Eul-Sa, Jeong-Sa.

午 O — the Horse

O (午) is midday fire, the horse at full gallop. As a day branch it adds passion, sociability and a need for freedom and movement. Cardinal (“peach-blossom”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Mi · clash: Ja · day pillars: Gyeong-O, Im-O, Gap-O, Byeong-O, Mu-O.

未 Mi — the Goat

Mi (未) is hot, dry summer earth that stores wood. As a day branch it adds gentleness on the outside, stubborn endurance inside and strong family feeling. Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: O · clash: Chuk · day pillars: Sin-Mi, Gye-Mi, Eul-Mi, Jeong-Mi, Gi-Mi.

申 Sin — the Monkey

Sin (申) is early-autumn metal carrying the seed of water. As a day branch it adds versatility, cleverness and a love of activity and travel. Starting (“post-horse”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Sa · clash: In · day pillars: Im-Sin, Gap-Sin, Byeong-Sin, Mu-Sin, Gyeong-Sin.

酉 Yu — the Rooster

Yu (酉) is pure autumn metal, the harvest blade and the jewel. As a day branch it adds precision, neatness and refined taste. Cardinal (“peach-blossom”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Jin · clash: Myo · day pillars: Gye-Yu, Eul-Yu, Jeong-Yu, Gi-Yu, Sin-Yu.

戌 Sul — the Dog

Sul (戌) is dry autumn earth that stores fire. As a day branch it adds loyalty, a protective instinct and a serious, sometimes spiritual streak. Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: Myo · clash: Jin · day pillars: Gap-Sul, Byeong-Sul, Mu-Sul, Gyeong-Sul, Im-Sul.

亥 Hae — the Pig

Hae (亥) is early-winter water holding the seed of wood. As a day branch it adds generosity, imagination and an easygoing, open heart. Starting (“post-horse”) branch.

Six-harmony partner: In · clash: Sa · day pillars: Eul-Hae, Jeong-Hae, Gi-Hae, Sin-Hae, Gye-Hae.

Harmonies and clashes

Six harmonies (육합): Ja–Chuk, In–Hae, Myo–Sul, Jin–Yu, Sa–Sin, O–Mi. Three harmonies (삼합) form elemental frames: Sin–Ja–Jin (Water), Hae–Myo–Mi (Wood), In–O–Sul (Fire), Sa–Yu–Chuk (Metal). Clashes (충) are opposite branches six places apart: Ja–O, Chuk–Mi, In–Sin, Myo–Yu, Jin–Sul, Sa–Hae.

In 2027 the year branch is Mi (the Goat), so O meets its six-harmony, Hae and Myo join a Wood triad, and Chuk meets a clash. See the 2027 section of each day pillar article.

Frequently asked questions

What are the twelve earthly branches?

Ja (Rat), Chuk (Ox), In (Tiger), Myo (Rabbit), Jin (Dragon), Sa (Snake), O (Horse), Mi (Goat), Sin (Monkey), Yu (Rooster), Sul (Dog) and Hae (Pig). Each also stands for a month, a two-hour period and a main element.

What are hidden stems?

Hidden stems (지장간) are the heavenly stems contained inside a branch, from one to three. For example Ja holds Im and Gye Water, and In holds Mu, Byeong and Gap. They let Saju count elements that are not visible on the surface.

Which branches clash?

Opposite branches clash: Ja–O, Chuk–Mi, In–Sin, Myo–Yu, Jin–Sul and Sa–Hae. In the day pillar a clash with the year branch is traditionally read as change in the spouse palace.

Is my zodiac animal my day branch?

Usually not. Your zodiac animal is your year branch. Your day branch comes from your birth date in the 60-day cycle and can be any of the twelve.
