Gyeong-Sin (庚申) Day Pillar: Personality, Love, Career & 2027 Outlook

Gyeong-Sin day pillar personality

  • decisive
  • loyal
  • strong-willed

Gyeong-Sin is Metal at Prosperity: stem and branch share an element. Tradition reads decisiveness, loyalty, strength of will and a strong sense of justice. Gyeong-Sin people are reliable and direct, and often excel where courage is needed. The classic caution is harshness: bluntness and inflexibility can create unnecessary conflict.

The Gyeong day master

Gyeong (庚) is raw metal: decisive, loyal and just. It cuts cleanly between right and wrong and moves quickly once a decision is made.

Sitting on Sin (申)

Sin (申) is early-autumn metal carrying the seed of water. As a day branch it adds versatility, cleverness and a love of activity and travel. Its main hidden stem, 庚, is Friend to a Gyeong day master (peer, self, independence), so the inner self leans toward peer.

Like every one of the 60 pillars, Gyeong-Sin pairs a stem and a branch of the same polarity, so it is a purely yang pillar: outgoing, initiating and direct in expression.

Hidden stems and the 12 life stages

Every earthly branch carries one to three hidden stems (지장간, jijanggan). Their share follows the traditional day-count table (월률분야) used by the site’s Saju calculator. Each hidden stem becomes a ten god when compared with the day master 庚.

Life stage: Prosperity (건록)

Prosperity (Geollok) is the mature adult earning a salary: self-reliant and capable. It suggests independence, steady competence and a dislike of depending on anyone. For Gyeong, that stage falls exactly on Sin, so the pillar’s own branch shows self-reliance.

Five-element balance

On its own, the Gyeong-Sin pillar is led by Metal (77%), followed by Earth (12%), Water (12%). Wood and Fire are absent from the pillar itself.

One pillar is only two of the eight characters in a full chart; the year, month and hour pillars often fill the gaps. Tradition treats under-represented elements as balancing themes, for example Wood → green, mornings, walks among trees, starting a course; Fire → warm light, exercise that makes you sweat, social plans; Earth → regular meals, routines, tidy spaces, gardening; Water → sleep, water, journaling, quiet reading.

Gyeong-Sin in love and compatibility

The day branch is called the spouse palace. With Friend in the spouse palace, partners are treated as equals and best friends. Independence is strong; a relationship works best with clear roles and shared goals rather than one person leading.

As a Gyeong day master, Gyeong-Sin is straightforward and loyal, protects a partner fiercely but may express care bluntly. Starting (“post-horse”) branch: In, Sin, Sa and Hae begin each season. Tradition links them with Yeokma (驛馬, travelling horse): movement, change of place and busy schedules.

Compatible and challenging day pillars

  • Best traditional match: Eul-Sa (乙巳) — stems combine (庚 + 乙 → Metal) and branches form a six-harmony (申 + 巳).
  • Friendly branches: Sa (Snake) (six-harmony) and Ja (子) and Jin (辰) (three-harmony Water frame).
  • Most tension: Gap-In (甲寅) — the branches clash (申 vs 寅) and the stems clash.

Compatibility in Saju compares whole charts; the day pillar is only one piece. Treat this as a conversation starter, not a verdict on any relationship.

Gyeong-Sin day pillar for women and men

Personality traits above apply to everyone. Gender enters only through an old convention: classical texts treat Wealth as the spouse star in a man’s chart and Officer in a woman’s chart. Modern readers apply the spouse palace to any partner.

Gyeong-Sin woman

No Officer star sits inside the Gyeong-Sin spouse palace, so for a woman’s chart classical texts read partner timing from the other pillars and the ten-year luck cycles. The palace’s main star, Friend, sets the tone of the relationship instead.

Gyeong-Sin man

No Wealth star sits inside the Gyeong-Sin spouse palace, so for a man’s chart classical texts read partner timing from the other pillars and the ten-year luck cycles. The palace’s main star, Friend, sets the tone of the relationship instead.

Gyeong-Sin career and work style

Career themes in Saju come from the day master and from the star in the spouse palace, which also shows how a person behaves in private and in partnerships.

  • From the Gyeong stem: military, police and law, engineering and manufacturing, surgery and technical trades, sports and operations.
  • From Friend in the day branch: self-employment, freelancing or any role with personal autonomy.
  • From the Prosperity stage: self-directed professional work with steady pay.

Work that draws on the pillar’s keywords (decisive, loyal, strong-willed) tends to feel most natural for Gyeong-Sin.

Traditional labels and concept notes

  • Stem-branch harmony (干如支同) — stem and branch share one element, so the self is strongly rooted: independence and persistence, sometimes stubbornness.
  • Starting (“post-horse”) branch — In, Sin, Sa and Hae begin each season. Tradition links them with Yeokma (驛馬, travelling horse): movement, change of place and busy schedules.
  • Nayin: Pomegranate Wood (石榴木), the “sound element” shared with Sin-Yu (辛酉).
  • Empty branches (gongmang, 空亡): Ja and Chuk (子丑), because Gyeong-Sin belongs to the decade that starts with 甲寅.
  • Same pillar as the years 1920, 1980, 2040 (for the year pillar, not the day).

Labels such as Goegang or Baekho come from classical star lists that vary between schools. They are historical vocabulary, not predictions.

Frequently asked questions about the Gyeong-Sin day pillar

What does the Gyeong-Sin (庚申) day pillar mean?

Gyeong-Sin is Yang Metal (庚) over the Monkey branch 申 (Metal). In Korean Saju tradition it is read as decisive, loyal and strong-willed, with the day master at the Prosperity (건록) stage of the 12 life stages. It is a cultural tradition enjoyed for entertainment, not a scientific prediction.

Which day pillars are compatible with Gyeong-Sin?

Traditionally the best match is Eul-Sa (乙巳), because the stems combine (庚–乙) and the branches form a six-harmony (申–巳). Day branches Ja and Jin are also friendly to Sin. The most tense pairing is Gap-In (甲寅), where the stems clash and the branches clash.

What are the hidden stems of the Gyeong-Sin day pillar?

The Sin branch holds 戊 (Mu, 7 days), 壬 (Im, 7 days), 庚 (Gyeong, 16 days). Relative to the Gyeong day master they are Indirect Resource, Eating God, Friend.

What does 2027 bring for Gyeong-Sin day pillar people?

In the 2027 Jeongmi (丁未) year the stem Jeong is Direct Officer for a Gyeong day master and the Mi branch’s main qi is Direct Resource. Sin and Mi have no major combination or clash; 2027 is read as a neutral background for the spouse palace. Read it as light entertainment, not advice.
