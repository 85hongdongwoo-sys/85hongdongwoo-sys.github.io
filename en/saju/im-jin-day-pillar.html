Im-Jin day pillar personality

  • visionary
  • powerful
  • charismatic

Im-Jin is the water dragon, one of the classical Goegang pillars. The Dragon earth is a reservoir of Water, so Im is both contained (Seven Killings) and supplied. Tradition reads vision, charisma and a powerful will, often with dramatic ups and downs. Im-Jin people are built for big projects; the advice is to respect the dam that channels all that water.

The Im day master

Im (壬) is the sea: broad, intelligent and freedom-loving. It sees the big picture, connects people and ideas, and dislikes being boxed in.

Sitting on Jin (辰)

Jin (辰) is damp spring earth that stores water, often linked with the dragon. As a day branch it adds ambition, imagination and a mix of practicality and big dreams. Its main hidden stem, 戊, is Seven Killings to a Im day master (pressure, courage, authority), so the inner self leans toward pressure.

Like every one of the 60 pillars, Im-Jin pairs a stem and a branch of the same polarity, so it is a purely yang pillar: outgoing, initiating and direct in expression.

Hidden stems and the 12 life stages

Every earthly branch carries one to three hidden stems (지장간, jijanggan). Their share follows the traditional day-count table (월률분야) used by the site’s Saju calculator. Each hidden stem becomes a ten god when compared with the day master 壬.

Life stage: Tomb (묘)

Tomb (Myo) is the storehouse where things are kept: thrift, patience and hidden reserves. It suggests someone who saves, plans and reveals their strength slowly. For Im, that stage falls exactly on Jin, so the pillar’s own branch shows storage and thrift.

Five-element balance

On its own, the Im-Jin pillar is led by Water (55%), followed by Earth (30%), Wood (15%). Fire and Metal are absent from the pillar itself.

One pillar is only two of the eight characters in a full chart; the year, month and hour pillars often fill the gaps. Tradition treats under-represented elements as balancing themes, for example Fire → warm light, exercise that makes you sweat, social plans; Metal → decluttering, deep breathing, clear decisions.

Im-Jin in love and compatibility

The day branch is called the spouse palace. With Seven Killings in the spouse palace, attraction to strong, intense personalities is common and the relationship carries some tension. Respecting each other’s space keeps the spark without the strain.

As a Im day master, Im-Jin is easygoing and open-minded, needs space and enjoys a partner who is also a friend. Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch: Jin, Sul, Chuk and Mi close each season and store its energy. Tradition links them with Hwagae (華蓋): reflection, study, art and spiritual interests.

Compatible and challenging day pillars

  • Best traditional match: Jeong-Yu (丁酉) — stems combine (壬 + 丁 → Wood) and branches form a six-harmony (辰 + 酉).
  • Friendly branches: Yu (Rooster) (six-harmony) and Sin (申) and Ja (子) (three-harmony Water frame).
  • Most tension: Byeong-Sul (丙戌) — the branches clash (辰 vs 戌) and the stems clash.

Compatibility in Saju compares whole charts; the day pillar is only one piece. Treat this as a conversation starter, not a verdict on any relationship.

Im-Jin day pillar for women and men

Personality traits above apply to everyone. Gender enters only through an old convention: classical texts treat Wealth as the spouse star in a man’s chart and Officer in a woman’s chart. Modern readers apply the spouse palace to any partner.

Im-Jin woman

Seven Killings (戊) sits in the spouse palace as the main qi, which classical texts read, for a woman’s chart, as a close and strong, visible bond: the partner star is “at home”. Being the main star, it tends to show clearly in daily life.

Im-Jin man

No Wealth star sits inside the Im-Jin spouse palace, so for a man’s chart classical texts read partner timing from the other pillars and the ten-year luck cycles. The palace’s main star, Seven Killings, sets the tone of the relationship instead.

Im-Jin career and work style

Career themes in Saju come from the day master and from the star in the spouse palace, which also shows how a person behaves in private and in partnerships.

  • From the Im stem: trade and logistics, strategy and consulting, IT and research, travel, media and international work.
  • From Seven Killings in the day branch: high-pressure roles: police, military, medicine, crisis management.
  • From the Tomb stage: finance, archives, property and long-term building.

Work that draws on the pillar’s keywords (visionary, powerful, charismatic) tends to feel most natural for Im-Jin.

For Im day masters Jeong (丁) is Direct Wealth and combines with Im (丁壬合, transforming toward Wood); Mi’s main qi is Direct Officer. Tradition reads steady income, attractive offers and relationship opportunities, with the caution that a combination can also tie you down.

Jin and Mi have no direct combination or clash; 2027 is read as a neutral background for the spouse palace, so the yearly stem’s ten-god matters more.

Im-Jin days in 2027: Jan 13, Mar 14, May 13, Jul 12, Sep 10, Nov 9. Some people like to schedule small personal milestones on their own pillar’s days, purely for fun.

Traditional labels and concept notes

  • Goegang (魁罡) — the "chief star" pillars: commanding, decisive, high-voltage; classically good for leadership roles, with a caution about extremes.
  • Storage (“flower-canopy”) branch — Jin, Sul, Chuk and Mi close each season and store its energy. Tradition links them with Hwagae (華蓋): reflection, study, art and spiritual interests.
  • Nayin: Long River Water (長流水), the “sound element” shared with Gye-Sa (癸巳).
  • Empty branches (gongmang, 空亡): O and Mi (午未), because Im-Jin belongs to the decade that starts with 甲申.
  • Same pillar as the years 1952, 2012, 2072 (for the year pillar, not the day).

Labels such as Goegang or Baekho come from classical star lists that vary between schools. They are historical vocabulary, not predictions.

Frequently asked questions about the Im-Jin day pillar

What does the Im-Jin (壬辰) day pillar mean?

Im-Jin is Yang Water (壬) over the Dragon branch 辰 (Earth). In Korean Saju tradition it is read as visionary, powerful and charismatic, with the day master at the Tomb (묘) stage of the 12 life stages. It is a cultural tradition enjoyed for entertainment, not a scientific prediction.

Which day pillars are compatible with Im-Jin?

Traditionally the best match is Jeong-Yu (丁酉), because the stems combine (壬–丁) and the branches form a six-harmony (辰–酉). Day branches Sin and Ja are also friendly to Jin. The most tense pairing is Byeong-Sul (丙戌), where the stems clash and the branches clash.

What are the hidden stems of the Im-Jin day pillar?

The Jin branch holds 乙 (Eul, 9 days), 癸 (Gye, 3 days), 戊 (Mu, 18 days). Relative to the Im day master they are Hurting Officer, Rob Wealth, Seven Killings.

What does 2027 bring for Im-Jin day pillar people?

In the 2027 Jeongmi (丁未) year the stem Jeong is Direct Wealth for a Im day master and the Mi branch’s main qi is Direct Officer. Jin and Mi have no direct combination or clash; 2027 is read as a neutral background for the spouse palace, so the yearly stem’s ten-god matters more. Read it as light entertainment, not advice.
