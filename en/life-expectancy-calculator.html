How it works

Baseline. Mortality by age follows a Gompertz–Makeham curve, μ(x) = A + B·eCx, originally fitted to Statistics Korea's 2024 complete life table (men: A = 0.00045481, B = 3.37193×10⁻⁶, C = 0.1198262; women: A = 0.00030512, B = 2.90104×10⁻⁷, C = 0.1420988). For each country and sex the page solves for a single factor k and uses μ(x) = k·A + √k·B·eCx, so that the model's life expectancy at birth matches the UN figure below to within 0.1 years (checked in your browser when the page loads). Scaling the age-independent part more than the old-age part reflects that countries with lower life expectancy lose more years to deaths at younger ages.

Health age converts your combined risk into years: since the risk of dying rises by about 12–14% for each year of age, a risk ratio R adds ln(R) ÷ C years. Doubling your risk adds roughly 5–6 years. Life expectancy is your current age plus your expected remaining years, assuming you have reached your current age, with the adjusted mortality rates.

Risk ratios are multiplied together and capped at 0.55–5× overall to allow for overlap between factors. Studies differ, so representative values are used. The model cannot capture child mortality separately, so in countries where many deaths happen in childhood (e.g. Nigeria) adult estimates are approximate.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a life expectancy calculator?
It gives a statistical average for people with similar habits and risk factors, not a prediction for you. Real lifespans vary widely, so treat the result as a guide to which habits matter most.
What is health age?
Health age is the age at which an average person of your sex has the same risk of dying as you do, given your habits and family history. Because mortality rises about 12–14% per year of age, doubling your risk adds roughly 5–6 years of health age.
Is my face photo uploaded?
No. The optional face-age estimate runs entirely in your browser with the open-source face-api model. The photo never leaves your device and is not used in the health age calculation.
