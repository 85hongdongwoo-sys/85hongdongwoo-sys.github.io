How the score works

MBTI chemistry (60%). We use the same rules as our MBTI compatibility calculator. Every pair starts at 30 points. Sharing S or N adds 25, because it shapes how two people talk about the world. Sharing T or F adds 14 (10 if different). Having different E/I letters adds 14 and different J/P letters adds 12 (6 each if they match), because opposite energy and planning styles can balance each other. That gives 52–95 points.

Korean zodiac match (40%). Koreans often ask “what's your 띠?”, meaning which of the 12 animals rules your birth year. Old folk tradition groups the animals into relationships. The rules match the compatibility table on our Korean age & zodiac table.

Idol-group roles. Each MBTI type gets a playful group position (leader, main vocal, main dancer, rapper, mood maker, producer and more), and each zodiac animal adds a stage trait. The younger of the two gets 막내 (maknae, the youngest member) energy. If you were born the same year, you're 동갑 (same-age) friends. The roles are generic labels and are not based on any real artist.

Frequently asked questions

How is the K-pop compatibility score calculated?
60% of the score comes from MBTI chemistry, using the same rules as our MBTI compatibility calculator (52–95 points). The other 40% comes from the Korean zodiac: three-harmony (삼합) pairs score 96, six-harmony (육합) 92, the same animal 80, no special tie 70, won-jin (원진) 52 and clashing (충) pairs 48.
Which Korean zodiac animal am I?
Your animal (띠) follows your birth year in a 12-year cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. The year changes at Lunar New Year (or at Ipchun, around February 4, in fortune-telling), so if you were born in January or early February you may belong to the previous year's animal.
Does this use real idols' data?
No. The idol-group roles (leader, main vocal, maknae and so on) are generic, playful labels based on personality type. The page contains no real artists, groups or agencies, and is not affiliated with any of them.
