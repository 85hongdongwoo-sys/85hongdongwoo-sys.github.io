10 LPA in hand: the short answer

A 10 LPA package is the point where many people expect to start paying tax — but in FY 2026-27 they do not. After employer PF, gratuity and the ₹75,000 standard deduction, taxable income is ₹8,40,962, far below the ₹12 lakh rebate limit. Take-home is about ₹71,122 a month, and the only deductions on your payslip are PF and professional tax.

Per month that means a gross salary of about ₹76,330 on the payslip, minus ₹5,000 of your own PF, ₹208 average professional tax and no TDS. Professional tax is actually deducted as ₹200 a month and ₹300 in February in Karnataka and Maharashtra.

CTC to in-hand: what gets deducted and why

Employer PF (₹60,000/yr) is part of your CTC but goes straight to the EPFO: 8.33% of basic (capped at ₹1,250 a month) into the pension scheme (EPS) and the rest into your EPF balance. Gratuity (₹24,038/yr) is a provision — you receive it only after five years of continuous service (one year for fixed-term employees under the labour codes). Many offer letters show both inside CTC, which is the main reason CTC ÷ 12 overstates take-home.

Employee PF (₹60,000/yr) is your 12% share, deducted every month. Income tax under the new regime is worked out on gross salary minus the ₹75,000 standard deduction: ₹9,15,962 − ₹75,000 = ₹8,40,962 taxable, fully covered by the rebate up to ₹12 lakh (section 87A under the old Act, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025 that applies from 1 April 2026).

If part of your 10 LPA CTC is variable pay

Suppose 10% of the package (₹1,00,000) is a performance bonus paid once a year. Basic, PF and HRA are then calculated on the fixed ₹9,00,000, so the monthly in-hand during the year is about ₹63,989, and the bonus month adds roughly ₹1,00,000 after its TDS (if paid at 100%). Over the full year that is ₹8,67,865 — +₹14,404 versus an all-fixed CTC, because PF is charged on a smaller basic.

Budgeting ₹71,100 a month

The 50/30/20 split is only a starting point — in Mumbai or Bengaluru rent alone can swallow most of the “needs” share at this income. Remember that you are already saving ₹10,000 a month through EPF (both shares), so your real savings rate is higher than the 20% shown.

Tips to get more in hand from 10 LPA

  • Negotiate on fixed pay. Every extra ₹1 lakh of CTC adds only about ₹7,133 a month to your in-hand at this level, so when negotiating, compare offers on fixed monthly in-hand, not CTC. Ask whether the CTC includes gratuity, insurance premiums, joining bonus or ESOPs — they inflate the headline without changing your monthly pay.
  • Employer NPS. Employer NPS (14% of basic, deductible in the new regime) saves no tax at 10 LPA because your tax is already nil — choose it only if you want extra retirement savings, as it would lower your monthly in-hand by about ₹5,833.
  • Tax-free reimbursements. Phone/internet bills and books/periodicals reimbursed against bills remain tax-free in both regimes; meal cards and LTA help only in the old regime.
  • Watch the cliff: if a raise pushes taxable income just above ₹12 lakh, marginal relief means the extra tax can never exceed the extra income — a hike never reduces your take-home.

FAQ: 10 LPA in hand salary

What is the in-hand salary for 10 LPA per month?

About ₹71,122 a month (₹8,53,462 a year) under the new tax regime in FY 2026-27, assuming basic pay is 50% of CTC, employer PF and gratuity are included in the CTC, PF is 12% of full basic and professional tax is ₹2,500 a year (Karnataka).

How much income tax is payable on 10 LPA in FY 2026-27?

Nil under the new regime. Taxable income is ₹8,40,962 after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, which is within the ₹12 lakh rebate limit.

Is the old or new tax regime better for 10 LPA?

The new regime. In our example with ₹1,50,000 of 80C, ₹25,000 of 80D and HRA exemption on ₹18,000/month rent, the old regime tax is ₹17,672 against ₹0 in the new regime. You would need about ₹22,500 of further deductions for the old regime to break even.

Why is 10 LPA not ₹83,333 per month in hand?

CTC includes money you never receive monthly: employer PF (₹60,000 a year) goes to your EPF account and the gratuity provision (₹24,038) is paid only when you leave after five years. Your own PF (₹60,000), professional tax and any TDS are then deducted from the gross salary of ₹9,15,962.

How much is 10 LPA per month before deductions?

10 LPA means ₹10 lakh per annum, or ₹83,333 per month as CTC. The gross monthly salary on your payslip is lower, about ₹76,330, because employer PF and gratuity are part of CTC.
