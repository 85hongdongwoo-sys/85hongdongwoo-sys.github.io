What changes in FY 2026-27

FY 2026-27 is the first tax year under the Income-tax Act 2025. The Union Budget 2026-27 kept the new-regime slabs, the ₹75,000 standard deduction and the rebate that makes taxable income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free. Combined with the standard deduction, a salaried employee pays no income tax up to ₹12.75 lakh of gross salary.

FAQ

What is the in-hand salary for 10 lakh CTC?

About ₹73,125 a month under the new regime with no income tax, after EPF and professional tax.

Up to what salary is there no income tax in FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime, taxable income up to ₹12 lakh is tax-free because of the rebate, so a salary up to ₹12.75 lakh (after the ₹75,000 standard deduction) pays no income tax.

Why is in-hand salary lower than CTC divided by 12?

CTC includes the employer EPF contribution, and your own EPF, professional tax and income tax are deducted before salary reaches your bank.
