5 lakh car loan EMI: quick answer

A ₹5 lakh car loan is the typical amount financed for an entry hatchback such as a ₹6–6.5 lakh on-road small car after a 20% down payment, or for a good used car. At a typical 9% new-car loan rate the EMI is ₹10,379 for 5 years and the interest adds up to ₹1,22,751. SBI’s new-car loans are currently priced from 8.55% for a credit score of 800+ to 9.5% for 650–699 (longer than 5 years), and private banks and dealer-tied lenders usually charge more. Car loans are typically fixed-rate, so the October 2026 repo hike does not change the EMI of a loan you already have.

Salary needed for a 5 lakh car loan: the 20/4/10 rule

A popular rule of thumb for buying a car is 20/4/10: put at least 20% down, finance for no more than 4 years, and keep the EMI (ideally all car costs) under 10% of your gross monthly income. For a ₹5 lakh loan over 4 years the EMI is ₹12,443, which means a gross income of about ₹1,24,400 a month to stay within 10%. Banks are far more lenient — at a 50% FOIR they would approve the 5-year EMI of ₹10,379 on a take-home of just ₹20,800 — but the bank’s limit is a ceiling, not a recommendation.

Prepaying a car loan

Paying ₹1,00,000 extra after two years would close the 5-year loan in 4 yrs and save ₹25,506 of interest. Check your agreement first: most car loans are fixed-rate, and lenders may charge a foreclosure or part-payment fee (commonly a few percent of the amount prepaid, sometimes waived after a lock-in). Since 1 January 2026, RBI rules bar prepayment charges on floating-rate loans to individuals, but that protection does not cover fixed-rate loans.

Tips

  • At this size, a 3-year loan costs only ₹5,521 more per month than a 5-year one and saves ₹50,355 of interest — often the smarter choice for a first car.
  • Get the loan sanctioned by your own bank before visiting the dealer — it gives you a benchmark to compare dealer finance against.
  • Car-loan interest is not tax-deductible for salaried individuals; only business use of the car allows claiming interest and depreciation as an expense.
  • Insurance, registration, accessories and extended warranty are often added to the loan. Pay for them upfront if you can — financing them means paying interest on items that lose value immediately.

FAQ: ₹5 lakh car loan EMI

What is the EMI for a ₹5 lakh car loan for 5 years?

About ₹10,379 a month at 9%, with total interest of ₹1,22,751.

What is the EMI for ₹5 lakh car loan for 7 years?

About ₹8,045 a month at 9%; total interest rises to ₹1,75,741.

What is the current SBI car loan interest rate?

SBI lists new-car loan rates from 8.55% to 9.5% depending on credit score and tenure (rate page updated 15 October 2026).

What salary is needed for a ₹5 lakh car loan?

Banks typically approve the 5-year EMI of ₹10,379 on a take-home of about ₹20,800 (50% FOIR). The more conservative 20/4/10 rule suggests a gross income of about ₹1,24,400 a month.

Can I prepay a car loan without charges?

It depends on the lender. Most car loans are fixed-rate and may carry foreclosure or part-payment charges; RBI’s ban on prepayment charges from 1 January 2026 covers floating-rate loans to individuals.
