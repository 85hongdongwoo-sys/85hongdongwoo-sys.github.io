About these EMI guides

Each article works out the EMI with the standard reducing-balance formula and the rates borrowers are actually being offered after the RBI’s October 2026 repo hike to 5.50%. For home loans we use 8% as a typical rate for a salaried borrower with a good credit score (SBI: 7.5%–8.8%; HDFC Bank: from 8%). Car loans use 9% (SBI 8.55%–9.5%) and personal loans 12%. Every page also shows other rates, so you can find the line that matches your sanction letter.

FAQ

What is the EMI for a ₹50 lakh home loan?

About ₹41,822 a month for 20 years at 8%, or ₹36,688 for 30 years.

What is the EMI per lakh for a home loan?

At 8%, about ₹836 per ₹1 lakh for 20 years and ₹734 for 30 years.

What are current home loan interest rates?

As of 3 November 2026, SBI’s regular home loans are priced at 7.5%–8.8% and HDFC Bank’s at 8%–13.45%, after the RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% on 7 October 2026.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), with P the loan, r the monthly rate and n the number of months.
