How the numbers are worked out

Every article uses the same verified inputs: Moneyfacts average fixed rates from late October 2026, the Bank of England’s 3.75% Bank Rate (held on 17 September 2026) plus an assumed 1-point tracker margin, the Stamp Duty Land Tax rates for England and Northern Ireland in force since 1 April 2025 (unchanged in the 28 October 2026 Budget), and the UK House Price Index for August 2026. Take-home pay comes from our UK salary after tax pages for 2026/27. Rates move daily — and the Bank of England announces its next decision on 5 November 2026 — so treat every figure as an estimate, not an offer.

FAQ

How do I calculate monthly mortgage repayments in the UK?

Repayment = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where r is the annual rate divided by 12 and n is the number of monthly payments. For interest-only, the payment is simply loan × annual rate ÷ 12. The calculator on this page does both.

How much is a £200,000 mortgage per month?

At the 5.99% average 5-year fixed rate (Moneyfacts, 28 October 2026), about £1,287 a month over 25 years and £1,139 over 35 years.

How much salary do I need for a £300k mortgage?

At the usual 4.5× income multiple you need about £66,667 of household income; at 5.5× about £54,545. Lenders then check your outgoings and stress-test the payment.

What are average UK mortgage rates now?

Moneyfacts put the average 2-year fix at 5.97% and the average 5-year fix at 5.99% on 28 October 2026; the average standard variable rate was 7.13%. Bank Rate is 3.75%, with the next Bank of England decision on 5 November 2026.

Are these figures a mortgage offer?

No. They are illustrative estimates using average rates. Your actual rate depends on your deposit, credit record, income and the lender, and fees can change the true cost.

Assumptions & sources

  • Average rates: Moneyfacts average 2-year fixed 5.97% and 5-year fixed 5.99% (all LTVs, 28 October 2026; 5.97% and 6.00% on 2 November) and average standard variable rate 7.13% (October 2026). Averages hide a wide spread — best-buy deals at 60% LTV can be well over half a point cheaper.
  • Bank of England Bank Rate 3.75% (held on 17 September 2026 by a 6–3 vote; next decision 5 November 2026). The tracker example adds an assumed 1.00% margin; best-buy trackers were around Bank Rate + 0.24 to + 0.60 points in early November 2026.
  • Repayments use the standard annuity formula with monthly compounding and assume the rate stays the same for the whole term — in reality fixed rates end after 2–5 years.
  • Stamp Duty Land Tax (England & Northern Ireland) from 1 April 2025: 0% to £125,000, 2% to £250,000, 5% to £925,000, 10% to £1.5m, 12% above; first-time buyers 0% to £300,000 and 5% to £500,000 (no relief above £500,000). Unchanged in the 28 October 2026 Budget. Scotland (LBTT) and Wales (LTT) use different taxes.
  • House prices: UK House Price Index for August 2026 (HM Land Registry/ONS, published 21 October 2026): UK £273,037, England £294,000, London £554,430, first-time buyers £230,097.
  • Take-home pay: 2026/27 income tax and NI for England, Wales & NI, no pension or student loan (see the UK salary pages).
