How a £82,500 salary is taxed in 2026/27

Income tax. After the £12,570 personal allowance, £69,930 is taxable: £37,700 at 20%, £32,230 at 40%. That is £20,432 of income tax — an effective rate of 24.8% on the full salary even though your top rate is 40%. £32,230 of your pay is above the £50,270 higher-rate threshold, where you pay 40% tax plus 2% NI.

National Insurance. Employees pay Class 1 NI of 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above, so £82,500 costs £3,661 a year (£305 a month). NI is the same in Scotland.

Scotland. Scottish taxpayers use six bands (19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48%). At £82,500 your top Scottish band is the advanced rate (45%) and you pay £22,857 income tax — £2,425 more than in the rest of the UK, leaving £55,982 a year.

Student loan and pension. A Plan 2 loan takes 9% of pay above £29,385, or £4,780 a year (£398 a month), cutting take-home to £53,627. A 5% workplace pension (£4,125) costs you only £2,475 in take-home under a net pay arrangement because it comes out before income tax, or £2,393 through salary sacrifice, which also saves NI.

A raise from here. A £1,000 pay rise to £83,500 adds £580 to take-home in England, Wales and NI — you keep 58p of each extra pound (marginal deduction rate 42%; 47% in Scotland).

FAQ

How much is £82,500 after tax in 2026/27?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland a £82,500 salary leaves £58,407 a year after £20,432 income tax and £3,661 National Insurance, with no pension or student loan. In Scotland the take-home is £55,982 because Scottish income tax is £22,857.

How much is £82,500 a year per month after tax?

About £4,867 a month in England, Wales and Northern Ireland and £4,665 a month in Scotland. Gross monthly pay is £6,875. With a Plan 2 student loan the monthly figure falls to £4,469.

What is £82,500 a year per hour after tax?

Based on a 37.5-hour week (1,950 hours a year), £82,500 is £42.31 an hour before tax and £29.95 an hour after income tax and NI (£28.71 in Scotland). Weekly take-home is £1,123.22.

What tax band is £82,500 in?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the top slice of £82,500 falls in the higher rate (40%). In Scotland it falls in the advanced rate (45%). Only the income inside each band is taxed at that band's rate.

How much student loan do I repay on £82,500?

On Plan 2 you repay 9% of income above £29,385, which is £4,780 a year or about £398 a month in 2026/27. Plan 1 (£26,900), Plan 4 (£33,795) and Plan 5 (£25,000) use different thresholds.
