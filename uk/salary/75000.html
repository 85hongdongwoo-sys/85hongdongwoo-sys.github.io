How a £75,000 salary is taxed in 2026/27

Income tax. After the £12,570 personal allowance, £62,430 is taxable: £37,700 at 20%, £24,730 at 40%. That is £17,432 of income tax — an effective rate of 23.2% on the full salary even though your top rate is 40%. £24,730 of your pay is above the £50,270 higher-rate threshold, where you pay 40% tax plus 2% NI.

National Insurance. Employees pay Class 1 NI of 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above, so £75,000 costs £3,511 a year (£293 a month). NI is the same in Scotland.

Scotland. Scottish taxpayers use six bands (19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48%). At £75,000 your top Scottish band is the higher rate (42%) and you pay £19,482 income tax — £2,050 more than in the rest of the UK, leaving £52,007 a year.

Student loan and pension. A Plan 2 loan takes 9% of pay above £29,385, or £4,105 a year (£342 a month), cutting take-home to £49,952. A 5% workplace pension (£3,750) costs you only £2,250 in take-home under a net pay arrangement because it comes out before income tax, or £2,175 through salary sacrifice, which also saves NI.

A raise from here. A £1,000 pay rise to £76,000 adds £580 to take-home in England, Wales and NI — you keep 58p of each extra pound (marginal deduction rate 42%; 47% in Scotland).

FAQ

How much is £75,000 after tax in 2026/27?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland a £75,000 salary leaves £54,057 a year after £17,432 income tax and £3,511 National Insurance, with no pension or student loan. In Scotland the take-home is £52,007 because Scottish income tax is £19,482.

How much is £75,000 a year per month after tax?

About £4,505 a month in England, Wales and Northern Ireland and £4,334 a month in Scotland. Gross monthly pay is £6,250. With a Plan 2 student loan the monthly figure falls to £4,163.

What is £75,000 a year per hour after tax?

Based on a 37.5-hour week (1,950 hours a year), £75,000 is £38.46 an hour before tax and £27.72 an hour after income tax and NI (£26.67 in Scotland). Weekly take-home is £1,039.57.

What tax band is £75,000 in?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the top slice of £75,000 falls in the higher rate (40%). In Scotland it falls in the higher rate (42%). Only the income inside each band is taxed at that band's rate.

How much student loan do I repay on £75,000?

On Plan 2 you repay 9% of income above £29,385, which is £4,105 a year or about £342 a month in 2026/27. Plan 1 (£26,900), Plan 4 (£33,795) and Plan 5 (£25,000) use different thresholds.
