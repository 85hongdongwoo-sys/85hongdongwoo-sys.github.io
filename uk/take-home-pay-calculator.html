FAQ

How much is £45,000 after tax in 2026/27?

About £2,993 a month (£35,920 a year) with no pension or student loan: £6,486 income tax and £2,594 National Insurance.

What is the personal allowance for 2026/27?

£12,570, frozen until 2031. It is reduced by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income above £100,000.

Does salary sacrifice save National Insurance?

Yes. Salary-sacrifice pension contributions reduce both income tax and National Insurance; net-pay and relief-at-source contributions only reduce income tax.
