How to get a student loan refund

Low annual income, a bonus month, the wrong plan code or paying past zero — all can mean money back.

You can claim a student loan refund from the Student Loans Company if repayments were taken when your income for the tax year was below your threshold, if you repaid more than you owed, if deductions started before you were due to repay, or if the wrong plan was used. Refunds are paid after the SLC has checked your income for the tax year with HMRC.

The four common reasons for a refund

  1. Your annual income was below the threshold. Payroll works month by month (or week by week), so overtime, a bonus or a back-dated pay rise can trigger a deduction even if your income for the whole tax year ends up under the yearly threshold.
  2. You repaid more than you owed. Employers keep deducting until told to stop, which can take a while after your balance reaches zero — especially if you also made voluntary payments.
  3. Deductions started too early. You are not due to repay until the April after you leave your course. Deductions taken earlier can be refunded.
  4. The wrong plan was used. For example, Plan 5 deductions on a Plan 2 loan (£395 a year too much on salaries above £29,385), or a Postgraduate Loan deduction you don’t owe.

Example: the bonus month

A Plan 2 graduate on £27,000 is paid £2,250.00 a month — below the £2,448 monthly threshold — so nothing is deducted for eleven months. In March they receive a £3,000 bonus, making that month’s pay £5,250.00. Payroll deducts 9% of the excess over the monthly threshold: £252. But their income for the whole year is £30,000, and 9% above £29,385 is only £55. They have overpaid by about £197 and can claim the difference back. If the bonus had taken them to under £29,385 for the year, the whole £252 would be refundable.

How to claim

  1. Wait until the tax year has ended (after 5 April) — the SLC needs your final annual income from HMRC.
  2. Sign in to your Student Loans Company online account and check the repayments recorded for the year. You can also phone the SLC.
  3. Request a refund and give your bank details. The SLC may ask for evidence such as your P60 or final payslip.
  4. The SLC checks your income with HMRC and pays the refund to your bank account. Refunds of tax-year overpayments are only made where repayments exceed what was due on your annual income.
Check every April. Compare your P60 “student loan deductions” with the annual figure for your salary on our salary pages. A gap of more than a few pounds is worth investigating.

Avoiding over-repayment at the end

When you are close to repaying in full, the SLC may offer to switch you from payroll deductions to Direct Debit for the final period so that payments stop exactly when the balance reaches zero. Keep your contact details up to date so you get that message. If you are making large voluntary payments, tell the SLC once the balance is cleared and ask your employer to stop deductions.

Self-employed and refunds

If you file a tax return, the student loan calculation is done on your annual income, so bonus-month over-deductions are usually corrected automatically: PAYE deductions are credited against the amount due. Where PAYE deductions exceed the total due for the year, HMRC or the SLC repays the excess. See self-employed student loan repayments.

Refunds after a loan is written off

If deductions continue after your write-off date — 25, 30 or 40 years depending on the plan (see when is my student loan written off?) — everything taken after that point should be refunded. The same applies to deductions after a loan is cancelled because of disability.

FAQ

Can I get a refund if I earned less than the student loan threshold?

Yes. If your total income for the tax year was below your plan’s annual threshold but repayments were deducted, you can ask the Student Loans Company for a refund after the tax year ends.

Why was student loan deducted from my bonus?

Employers work out repayments for each pay period on its own. A bonus can push one month above the monthly threshold even if your yearly income is lower, so a deduction is made. You can claim back any amount above what was due on your annual income.

How do I claim a student loan refund?

After 5 April, contact the Student Loans Company through your online account or by phone, check the repayments recorded and request a refund to your bank account. You may need your P60.

What if I repaid more than I owed?

The Student Loans Company refunds any amount paid after the balance reached zero. Ask your employer to stop deductions once the SLC confirms the loan is repaid.

Can I get a refund if the wrong plan type was used?

Yes. Tell your employer and the Student Loans Company. The SLC will correct the record and refund any over-deductions.
