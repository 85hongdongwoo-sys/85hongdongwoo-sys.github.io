FAQ

How much tax do I pay on £40,000 dividends?

With a £12,570 salary, about £4,821 in 2026/27: £500 is tax-free, £37,200 is taxed at 10.75% and the last £2,300 at 35.75%.

Are dividends in an ISA taxed?

No. Dividends and gains inside a stocks and shares ISA or pension are tax-free.
