£13.21 an hour is how much a year?

At 37.5 hours a week, £13.21 an hour is about £25,760 a year, or £2,147 a month before tax. At 40 hours it is about £27,477 a year.

FAQ

What is the minimum wage in April 2027?

£13.21 an hour for workers aged 21 and over, £11.27 for 18–20 year olds and £8.31 for 16–17 year olds and apprentices.

When does the new minimum wage start?

On 1 April 2027.

How much is £13.21 an hour per year full time?

About £25,760 a year at 37.5 hours a week (£13.21 × 37.5 × 52).

Will income tax thresholds rise too?

No. The personal allowance stays frozen at £12,570 until 2031, so more of the rise is taxed.
